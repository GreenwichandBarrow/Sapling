## What not to over-share

- Do not use "search fund," "ETA," "committed equity," or "24-month window" language. Use "holding company" / "long-term investment firm."
- Do not share active target names, revenue, EBITDA, margin, or LOI detail.
- Keep geography and buy-box high-level.
- Do not turn this into a capital-raise or lender pitch; relationship before ask.
