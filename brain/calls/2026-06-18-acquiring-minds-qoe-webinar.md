## Notes

Acquiring Minds webinar hosted by [[entities/will-smith|Will Smith]], featuring [[entities/max-loomis|Max Loomis]] of [[entities/lcs|LCS]]. The session framed QoE as a diligence tool for asking whether earnings are economically real, repeatable, and financeable after acquisition, not just whether they were historically accurate.

Key points:
- Revenue quality must be decomposed into volume, pricing, and one-time project effects.
- Customer concentration can become a deal killer, with nuance for diversified public-company accounts.
- EBITDA is only useful if it converts into cash; capex and working capital matter.
- Owner perks and hidden wage catch-up are common adjustment or post-close risk areas.
