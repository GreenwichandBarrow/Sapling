- Seller is 76, founder’s daughter, ready to transition; none of her 3 kids want to run it
- Business has been around 80 years, already survived key-man risk (founder’s name, father’s brand)
- Strong brand DNA: 5 core styles iterated over decades, purpose-driven ethos (women, arts, children)
- Prime distribution: Madison Avenue flagship, Chicago original location, Bergdorf Goodman wholesale
  - Bergdorf throwing an 80th anniversary party for the brand later this month
- Price points are ultra-high-end: bracelets \~$30K, necklaces commonly \~$100K
- EBITDA margin at 18%; best-in-class is 30%, so meaningful room for improvement
- Top-line growth only \~3%; Kay sees no reason it shouldn’t be a $60M+ business
