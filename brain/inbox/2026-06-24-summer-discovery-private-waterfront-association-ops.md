# Summer discovery - private waterfront association operations pain map

## Description
Capture for summer field discovery, not yet a standalone niche.

Signal from Kay: deeded neighborhood beaches and small private beach associations on Long Island historically relied on member participation for upkeep and governance. Newer generations have less time, bandwidth, and interest in volunteer management, so operational issues persist.

Working framing: this is a promising customer pain map that may point to a better target category, not yet a standalone niche.

Potential operating pain areas:
- Trash removal and seasonal cleanup
- Sand, grass, landscaping, and erosion-related upkeep
- Dock, mooring, playground, access-gate, and shared-amenity maintenance
- Reservation scheduling for member parties and gatherings
- Permits, insurance, rules enforcement, and vendor coordination
- Board / member communications and annual operating calendar

Discovery question:
Could a search-fundable B2B service niche emerge from recurring compliance and facility-risk services for deeded beaches, yacht clubs, private waterfront associations, private clubs, and luxury residential communities?

Adjacent categories to test:
- Water / environmental testing
- Fire / life-safety inspection
- Pest management
- Community association management
- Dock / marina maintenance
- Private amenity operations management
