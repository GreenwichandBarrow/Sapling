# Queue Berkshire and luxury-trend recurring business model proxies for niche intelligence

The task is to identify recurring, sticky, high-margin, compliance/vital, or growth-supported models that may translate into search-scale niches with G&B fit.

Promising proxy areas to test during the next Monday Niche Intelligence run:
