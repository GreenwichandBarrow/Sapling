# G&B Website Repositioning Brief

## Objective

Rebuild the Greenwich & Barrow website around the firm’s long-duration holding company identity. The site should communicate that G&B is a permanent, thoughtful home for enduring businesses built on heritage, quality, and community.

## Core Positioning Line

A trusted home for your business, rooted in heritage, community, and quality.

## Design Direction

Minimal, editorial, quiet. More Chanel annual report / private family office than startup landing page. Large white space, restrained typography, subtle G&B mark, no loud claims, no decorative excess.

## Guardrails

- No portfolio page.
- No SG mention.
- No investor page.
- No heavy Kay bio.
- No “we are acquiring companies” language on the homepage.
- No over-explanation of luxury.
