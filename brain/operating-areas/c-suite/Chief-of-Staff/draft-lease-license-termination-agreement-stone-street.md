# Mutual Termination and Surrender Agreement

This Mutual Termination and Surrender Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of August 31, 2026 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Stone Street Software, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, having an address at One Hanover Square, New York, New York ("Licensor"), and Greenwich & Barrow LLC, a [Delaware/New York] limited liability company, having an address at [LICENSEE NOTICE ADDRESS] ("Licensee"). Licensor and Licensee may be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

## 1. Original Agreement

The Parties are parties to that certain License Agreement effective as of [ORIGINAL EFFECTIVE DATE], pursuant to which Licensor licensed to Licensee certain desk, office, or partial office space, together with certain furniture, fixtures, and equipment, at Unit 1 in the building known as One Hanover Square, New York, NY (the "License Agreement"). The License Agreement references that certain Sublease Agreement dated as of June 14, 2024 between MTP - One Hanover Square Leasehold, LLC, as landlord, and Licensor, as tenant.

The Parties acknowledge that the original stated term of the License Agreement was twelve (12) months, commencing March 1, 2025. The Parties further acknowledge that Licensee continued to occupy or use the License Premises after the expiration of the original stated term, and the Parties now desire to fully terminate and settle Licensee's continued occupancy and any related obligations.

Capitalized terms used but not defined in this Agreement have the meanings given to them in the License Agreement.

## 2. Mutual Termination

The Parties agree that Licensee's continued occupancy, use, and rights, if any, under or following the License Agreement are terminated by mutual agreement effective as of August 31, 2026 (the "Termination Date"), except for any provisions that expressly survive termination or that are expressly preserved in this Agreement.

As of the Termination Date, Licensee has no further right to occupy or use the License Premises, except as reasonably necessary to retrieve any remaining personal property with Licensor's prior coordination and consent.

## 3. Rent and Other Charges

Licensor confirms that Licensee is not required to pay Base Rent for September 2026.

Except as expressly stated in this Agreement, each Party confirms that, as of the Effective Date, no rent, additional rent, IT fees, utilities, operating charges, late fees, penalties, or other amounts are due from Licensee to Licensor under the License Agreement.

## 4. Security Deposit and Prepaid Amounts

The Parties acknowledge that Licensee paid $4,500 at signing in connection with the License Agreement. The Parties further acknowledge that Exhibit B to the License Agreement provided that the Security Deposit would pay for the first month and the last two months of Base Rent, and that Licensee would begin regular monthly payment of Base Rent on or before the first day of the second month of the Term. The Parties further acknowledge that Exhibit B listed an IT Fee in connection with the total amount due at signing.


The Parties agree that the $4,500 paid at signing consisted of: (i) $1,000 applied to the first month of Base Rent for March 2025, (ii) $2,000 designated for the last two months of Base Rent, and (iii) a $1,500 IT Fee. The $1,000 applied to March 2025 has been used and is not refundable.

The Parties further acknowledge the payment history summarized on Schedule 1 attached hereto, which reflects regular $1,000 monthly rent payments after March 2025, including payments corresponding to January 2026 and February 2026, which were the final two months of the original twelve-month Term.

Accordingly, Licensor shall return to Licensee $2,000, representing the unapplied portion of the Security Deposit designated for the last two months of Base Rent, less only any amounts specifically identified in writing by Licensor and agreed by Licensee as properly chargeable under the License Agreement or this Agreement.

The Parties acknowledge that the refundability of the $1,500 IT Fee is not clear from the License Agreement. To the extent any portion of the IT Fee was collected as part of a refundable security deposit, prepaid amount, or otherwise remains unused or refundable, Licensor shall also return that unused or refundable portion to Licensee. If Licensor contends the IT Fee is non-refundable or was fully earned, Licensor shall confirm that position in writing.

For clarity, if Licensor contends that any portion of the $2,000 return amount was previously applied or should be withheld, Licensor shall identify in writing: (i) the amount applied or withheld, (ii) the month, invoice, charge, or obligation to which it was applied or for which it is being withheld, and (iii) the remaining balance due back to Licensee.

Licensor shall return the agreed unused or refundable amounts to Licensee within five (5) business days after the Effective Date by check, ACH, wire transfer, or such other payment method as the Parties may agree in writing.

Except for the refund obligation described in this Section 4 and any obligations expressly preserved in this Agreement, the Parties confirm that no rent, additional rent, IT fees, utilities, operating charges, late fees, penalties, or other amounts are due from Licensee to Licensor under the License Agreement or Licensee's continued post-term occupancy.

## 7. Mutual Release

Subject to Section 8 below, each Party, on behalf of itself and its successors and assigns, releases the other Party and its members, managers, officers, employees, agents, representatives, successors, and assigns from any and all claims, demands, damages, liabilities, costs, expenses, obligations, and causes of action, whether known or unknown, arising out of or relating to the License Agreement, the License Premises, or the termination of Licensee's occupancy, through the Effective Date.

## 8. Carve-Outs

Nothing in this Agreement releases or limits any claim or obligation arising from:

1. a Party's breach of this Agreement;
2. fraud, intentional misconduct, or willful misconduct;
3. obligations that expressly survive termination under the License Agreement, but only to the extent arising from facts or events not released under Section 7;
4. third-party claims arising from Licensee's use or occupancy of the License Premises before the Termination Date; or
5. any obligations owed to MTP - One Hanover Square Leasehold, LLC or required under the master sublease or building rules, to the extent those obligations cannot be waived by Licensor and Licensee.
