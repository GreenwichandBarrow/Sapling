# 02 - Sourcing System Conversion Diagnosis

Status: Complete based on available G&B trackers, skills, and internal call notes.

Purpose: diagnose why the current sourcing system is not reliably producing seller conversations, financials, and LOIs, and define what must change.

## Evidence Reviewed

- Weekly Activity Tracker, including Quarterly Summary and Weekly Topline metrics.
- Industry Research Tracker, including WEEKLY REVIEW and DEALSX tabs.
- Current skill architecture: Good Morning, Niche Intelligence, Outreach Manager, Deal Aggregator, Weekly Tracker.
- G&B deal history notes, including Hangman Fine Arts and Acumen.
- Investor/search benchmark memory files.

## Core Diagnosis

The system is not broken because G&B lacks ideas or activity. It is breaking because the weekly system does not reliably convert thesis signals into routed outreach, and it does not reliably convert owner replies into live qualification and financials.

The highest-impact break is after thesis selection and before owner conversion.

## What the Data Says

- Fact: From the Weekly Topline, G&B has weeks with 3 to 8 meaningful owner conversations, so owner conversations are possible when the system is active.
- Fact: Financials received are sporadic. Several weeks show zero financials even when owner conversations occur.
- Fact: LOIs submitted and signed are zero in the reviewed weekly period.
- Fact: The Industry Research Tracker contains many Under Review or New niches, while active outreach lanes are concentrated in a smaller set.
- Fact: The DealsX tab already contains structured verticals, subverticals, keywords, active flags, and intended outreach channels.
- Fact: The Niche Intelligence skill already requires Monday full runs, Thursday light scans, one-pagers, scorecards, channel recommendations, and Good Morning surfacing.
- Fact: The Outreach Manager skill already assigns Kay, Camilla, DealsX, and Codex distinct roles.

## Where the System Breaks

### 1. Thesis signals are not always converted into channel decisions

Fact: The Niche Intelligence skill says a channel decision must happen before target build or outreach. It also says Deal 1 Activate/Test requires an explicit tailwind or growth trend.

Inference: When thesis work does not appear in Good Morning or fails silently, Kay has to remember to restart the thesis engine manually. That makes the system dependent on Kay's attention rather than on the process.

Required change: Good Morning must surface two thesis items every week:
- Tuesday: output from Monday-night full Niche Intelligence run.
- Friday: urgent/queue/park output from Thursday-night light scan.

If either strategic cycle fails, Good Morning must show a red decision item the next morning.

### 2. DealsX owner replies are creating a delay point

Fact: Kay said there are 10 owner responses in the DealsX inbox that she has been unable to get to.

Fact: Kay already receives automated email alerts from DealsX when an owner responds.

Inference: Alerts alone do not create conversion. The missing step is triage and next-step routing.

Required change: DealsX should classify every owner reply within 24 hours as:
- FYI / admin
- Not interested
- Interested

Interested replies should be flagged as:
- Wants more information
- Needs clarifying before live time
- Wants to schedule a call
- Asks to receive a call

Camilla should own initial cold qualifier calls. Kay should enter only when seller trust, taste, strategic conviction, or high-stakes relationship work matters.

### 3. First-call qualification is not yet a protected lane

Fact: Kay told Camilla she should do the first call on cold proprietary responses.

Inference: This is directionally right, but it needs a standing operating role, not an ad hoc ask.

Required change: Camilla's sourcing role should be:
- review Interested replies routed by DealsX
- handle first cold qualifier call
- confirm size, seriousness, and transition logic
- escalate to Kay only if the owner is meaningfully live

Kay's role should not be first-call qualification on cold replies.

### 4. Seller conversations are not consistently pushed to financials

Fact: Weekly metrics show meaningful owner conversations can happen, but financials do not consistently follow.

Inference: The conversion script after first call is under-systematized. The system needs a standard "self-opt-out" framing and a clean financials ask.

Required change: Every qualified call should end in one of four statuses:
- Too small / no transition
- Relationship nurture
- Send light information first
- Ask for financials / NDA

The call owner must leave the call with a next action and date. If no next action exists, the call should be treated as a pass or nurture, not an open loop.

### 5. Pipeline work gets displaced by urgent strategic or deal work

Fact: SG deck/model work displaced normal weekly sourcing cycles.

Inference: This is predictable. A live deal will always pull attention. The process must protect a minimum pipeline engine even during live deal work.

Required change: Run two tracks every week:
- Track A: live deal / deck / investor work.
- Track B: minimum pipeline maintenance.

Track B minimums:
- one active thesis reviewed or advanced
- owner replies triaged within 24 hours
- Monday/Thursday follow-ups handled
- one target list or channel decision advanced

## Stop / Start / Continue

### Stop

- Stop letting owner replies sit with Kay.
- Stop sending niches to outreach without a clear channel decision.
- Stop relying on Kay to notice skill failures.
- Stop treating research/deck work as a reason to pause sourcing entirely.

### Start

- Start DealsX 24-hour response triage.
- Start Camilla cold qualifier role formally.
- Start Good Morning red flags for failed thesis cycles.
- Start a weekly channel-decision slot in Good Morning.
- Start a "financials conversion" status after every owner call.

### Continue

- Continue using DealsX for list build, ranking, and scaled cold outreach.
- Continue Kay-led warm outreach where her background materially improves conversion.
- Continue Monday full Niche Intelligence and Thursday light signal scan.
- Continue using the dashboard as the central operating view.
- Continue Friday systems/finance/nonprofit day, with no new outreach unless urgent.

## Proposed Weekly Sourcing Ceremony

Sunday:
- Review active theses, conference pipeline, and what must be queued for the week.

Monday:
- Launch or refresh outreach.
- Send direct/warm follow-ups that need the full week for response.
- Monday night: Niche Intelligence full run.

Tuesday:
- Good Morning surfaces thesis/channel recommendations.
- Kay decides Activate / Test / Hold / Kill.
- Camilla handles first-call qualification for active replies.

Wednesday:
- Review live paths, financials received, and calls scheduled.
- Push qualified conversations toward NDA or financials.

Thursday:
- Follow up Monday direct outreach.
- Thursday night: light thesis signal scan.

Friday:
- Systems, tracker health, finance, nonprofit.
- No broad outreach unless there is a live deal or seller-sensitive reason.

## Bottom Line

The sourcing system should become a repeatable owner-conversion machine, not a research machine. The immediate improvement is not more activity. It is stricter routing:

Thesis signal -> channel decision -> target list -> outreach -> reply triage -> Camilla qualifier -> Kay seller conversation -> financials -> LOI.
