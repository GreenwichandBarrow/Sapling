# 05 - Repeatable Owner-Conversion System

Status: Complete.

Purpose: design the G&B owner-conversion system from thesis to LOI, assigning each step to Kay, Camilla, DealsX, and Codex. The system is designed around Kay's strengths: trust-building, judgment, taste, synthesis, seller conviction, and high-context relationship work.

## Design Principle

Kay should not be the system's bottleneck.

Kay enters when the moment requires:
- trust
- taste
- judgment
- conviction
- seller relationship
- investor confidence
- final decision

Kay should not own:
- inbox triage
- CRM hygiene
- cold-reply sorting
- low-fit first calls
- target-list cleanup
- repeated follow-up mechanics

## Full Process Map

1. Thesis signal
2. Niche one-pager and scorecard
3. Kay decision: Activate / Test / Hold / Kill
4. Channel decision
5. Target universe
6. Target ranking and warm-path check
7. Outreach path
8. Reply triage
9. First screen
10. Kay seller conversation
11. NDA / financials
12. Model and deal screen
13. Investor/lender pressure test
14. LOI

## Channel Rules

DealsX cold channel:
- Use when target universe is broad enough, warm-path advantage is limited, and messaging can be systematized.

Kay warm channel:
- Use when Kay has a relationship, shared background, high-status credibility, or a reason the owner would respond differently to her personally.

Camilla qualifier channel:
- Use after owner reply from cold proprietary outreach, before Kay spends live time.

Conference / association channel:
- Use when niche is small, relationship-driven, trust-based, or hard to reach by email.

No-go:
- Use when no target density, no tailwind, no revenue-quality path, or no clear channel exists.

## Weekly Operating Ceremony

Sunday:
- Codex reviews active theses, conference pipeline, and which channels need work.
- Kay reviews only the decisions that matter.

Monday:
- Launch priority outreach and direct follow-ups.
- Send anything that benefits from a full response week.
- Monday night Niche Intelligence full run.

Tuesday:
- Good Morning surfaces new thesis/channel decisions.
- Kay makes Activate / Test / Hold / Kill decisions.
- Camilla handles cold qualifier calls.
- Kay handles warm and high-trust seller outreach.

Wednesday:
- Review live paths and owner responses.
- Push qualified conversations toward NDA or financials.
- Clear reply backlog.

Thursday:
- Follow up Monday warm/direct outreach.
- Escalate interested owner replies not yet handled.
- Thursday night light thesis signal scan.

Friday:
- Systems day.
- No broad email/outreach unless seller-sensitive.
- Review metrics, failed skills, tracker hygiene, finance, nonprofit.

## Failure Triggers

Red next-morning Good Morning item:
- Niche Intelligence full run failed.
- Thursday signal scan failed.
- DealsX Interested reply unclassified for more than 24 hours.
- Interested reply not assigned to Camilla or Kay by next business day.
- Qualified owner conversation has no next action.
- Financials were expected but not requested.

Yellow Good Morning item:
- No new thesis queued to DealsX for two weeks.
- Target list is more than seven days old and not reviewed.
- Owner conversations below two per week.
- No financials received for two consecutive weeks.
- Outreach channel missing on an active niche.

## What Makes This More Like Kay

- Kay is not forced into high-volume cold calling.
- Kay does not live in CRM maintenance.
- Kay uses her highest-value skills: reading people, building trust, sensing fit, shaping the strategic frame, and making final calls.
- Camilla learns sourcing through real first-call reps, not abstract research.
- DealsX handles volume, reply classification, and list mechanics.
- Codex holds the system together, watches for failures, and turns signals into decisions.

## Success Metrics

Weekly:
- Owner replies triaged within 24 hours.
- 2 to 5 meaningful owner conversations.
- At least one active thesis advanced, killed, or routed.
- At least one live path pushed toward financials or explicitly closed.

Monthly:
- 2 to 4 financial packages received.
- 1 to 2 serious live seller conversations.
- 1 qualified LOI candidate or a clear kill decision.

## Bottom Line

The G&B sales system should not be a generic search-fund outreach process. It should be a repeatable owner-conversion process where the machine creates qualified moments, and Kay enters when her judgment changes the outcome.
