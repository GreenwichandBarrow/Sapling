# Private Family Office

Quiet venture repository for a potential self-serve digital library around modern family-office systems for the home.

## Working Premise

Build a clean, organic-minimalist, academically framed digital library for women managing home, family, documents, valuables, money flows, and legacy.

This is not a personal brand, not G&B, not a consulting practice, and not a client-service business.

## Constraints

- No public-facing Kay persona
- No direct client work
- No custom setup
- No social-media dependency as the core model
- Keep separate from G&B/search fund materials
- Build as an owned digital asset: website, library, templates, resources, and potential affiliate/referral economics

## Initial Shelves

- Household systems
- Children and school administration
- Documents and records
- Insurance and advisors
- Valuables, heirlooms, and collections
- Money flows
- Health and home environment
- Travel
- Legacy and stewardship
