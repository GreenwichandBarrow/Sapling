Identity Documents Checklist

A reference list for gathering the identity records that establish who each person is and how the household is legally connected.

Secure storage: keep the original or current copy in a protected household document organizer. Italic timing shows the review rhythm.

Citizenship certificates
Naturalization records
Military discharge papers, if relevant
