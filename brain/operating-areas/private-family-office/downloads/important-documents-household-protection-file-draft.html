How To Use This File

Build the system section by section.

This file is designed to be completed over time. Start with the documents that are easiest to identify, then return to the registers as records are found, renewed, created, or updated.

For original documents, use a protected physical location that the right person can locate. For digital records, record where access is managed rather than writing sensitive passwords into an unsecured printable.

Identity Documents

Checklist

Identity documents are the records that establish who each person is and how the household is legally connected. They should have a clear physical home, and the right person should know where to find them.

Secure storage: keep the original or current copy in a protected household document organizer.

Birth certificates

Review Rhythm

Keep the system current.

Monthly
New documents, newly received statements, address or household changes.
Quarterly
Recurring household records, open claims, major vendor or service changes.
Annually
Insurance policies, beneficiaries, tax records, school forms, household continuity notes.
Multiyear
Passports, estate documents, long-term IDs, major property records.
Event-based
Birth, adoption, marriage, divorce, move, name change, new dependent, property purchase, major diagnosis, death, or material financial change.
