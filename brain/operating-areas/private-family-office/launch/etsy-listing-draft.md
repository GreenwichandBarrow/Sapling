# Etsy Listing Draft

This is a draft only. Do not publish without Kay's approval.

## Strategic Use

Etsy may be useful for a near-term search-intent test because buyers already search for printable household binders, important document organizers, emergency binders, and just-in-case files.

The Private Family Office brand should not become Etsy-native. The listing should use marketplace search vocabulary where needed while keeping the product language calm and organized.

## Draft Title

Important Documents Binder Printable, TPFO Household Protection Binder, Family Document Organizer, Identity Documents Checklist, Home Records PDF

## Draft Short Description

A minimalist printable system for organizing important household documents, insurance details, access notes, and continuity instructions.

## Draft Long Description

The TPFO Household Protection Binder is a printable organizational system for the process-oriented person managing the financial and operational responsibilities of a modern family office.

Use it to gather important records, note where originals and copies are stored, track renewal timing, and create a clearer household reference file.

Included pages:

- Identity Documents Checklist
- Important Documents Inventory
- Insurance Policy Register
- Legal & Estate Document Register
- Emergency Information Sheet
- Digital Access Locator
- Property & Asset Records Register
- Beneficiaries & Account Access Register
- Continuity Instructions Starter
- Review Rhythm reference page

This is a digital download. No physical product will be shipped.

This product is an organizational resource. It is not legal, tax, insurance, investment, medical, or financial advice.

## Risk Notes

- Etsy search vocabulary often skews crisis-oriented. Use necessary marketplace terms for discovery, but keep the product itself framed as household organization.
- Etsy is not owned distribution. Use it as a test channel, not the long-term home of the brand.
- Kay approval required before listing fees, public launch, or payment enablement.
