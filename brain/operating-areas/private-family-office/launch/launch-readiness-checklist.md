## Recommended Pricing Test

This is a recommendation for discussion, not a saved decision.

### Initial Test Price

Test at **$37** or **$49**.

Reason:

- Low enough for an impulse digital-product purchase.
- High enough to test whether the product is valued beyond a free checklist.
- More credible than a very low-price printable while the brand is positioned as serious and organized.

### Revenue Reality

At $37, $10,000/month gross would require roughly 271 purchases before fees, refunds, taxes, and operating costs.

At $49, $10,000/month gross would require roughly 205 purchases before fees, refunds, taxes, and operating costs.

At $79, $10,000/month gross would require roughly 127 purchases before fees, refunds, taxes, and operating costs.

This means the first launch should be treated as a learning test, not a dependable income bridge.
