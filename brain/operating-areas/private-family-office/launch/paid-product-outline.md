# Paid Product Outline

Working title: **Important Documents / Household Protection File**

## Product Promise

A clean, self-serve system for gathering, labeling, and maintaining the documents and instructions that help a household stay organized.

This is not legal, tax, insurance, medical, or financial advice. It is an organizational product.

## Primary Buyer

A systems-oriented person who is newly managing, or newly formalizing, the financial and operational responsibilities of a modern family office.

## Product Format

Recommended first version:

- PDF printable.
- Letter-size pages.
- Minimalist neutral design matching the website.
- Checklists and registers that can be printed and placed in a physical document organizer.
- No custom setup.
- No personal advisory component.

## Proposed Contents

### Cover

Title, subtitle, date/version, and Private Family Office brand mark.

### How To Use This File

Short orientation explaining that the reader can print the pages, complete them over time, and store completed records in a protected household document organizer.

### Master Contents

A clean table of contents organized by envelope:

- Identity Documents
- Insurance Policies
- Legal & Estate Documents
- Emergency Information
- Digital Access
- Property & Asset Records
- Beneficiaries & Account Access
- Continuity Instructions

### Identity Documents Checklist

The free sample asset, included inside the paid product.

Sections:

- Documents You Receive or Create Once
- Documents You Need to Renew or Keep Current

Visual markers:

- Secure-storage icon after documents that should usually live in a protected physical organizer.
- Quiet italic review rhythm next to relevant document types.

### Important Documents Inventory

A one-page register for recording what exists, where it is stored, and who knows how to locate it.

Fields:

- Document
- Person
- Original location
- Copy location
- Renewal or review timing
- Notes

### Insurance Policy Register

A household-level register, not an insurance advice worksheet.

Fields:

- Policy type
- Carrier
- Policy number
- Named insured
- Renewal date
- Agent or contact
- Storage location
- Notes

### Legal & Estate Document Register

Fields:

- Document
- Date signed
- Attorney or preparer
- Original location
- Copy location
- Review trigger
- Notes

### Emergency Information Sheet

Fields:

- Household members
- Emergency contacts
- Doctors
- Preferred hospital
- Allergies and critical medical notes
- Pet care notes if relevant
- Important property access notes

### Digital Access Locator

This should not ask readers to write passwords directly into an unsecured printable.

Fields:

- Account or system
- Where access is managed
- Who should be able to locate access
- Recovery email or phone if appropriate
- Notes

### Property & Asset Records Register

Fields:

- Property or asset
- Ownership record
- Insurance connection
- Appraisal or valuation location
- Warranty or service records
- Notes

### Beneficiaries & Account Access Register

Fields:

- Account or policy
- Institution
- Beneficiary status confirmed
- Last reviewed
- Contact or access notes

### Continuity Instructions Starter

Plain-language prompts for the person who may need to keep the household running.

Prompts:

- Bills and recurring obligations
- School or dependent-care routines
- Household vendors
- Insurance contacts
- Property access
- Where key documents live
- What should be reviewed first

### Review Rhythm

A concise reference page grouping review timing by cadence:

- Monthly: new documents, newly received statements, address or household changes.
- Quarterly: recurring household records, open claims, major vendor or service changes.
- Annually: insurance policies, beneficiaries, tax records, school forms, household continuity notes.
- Multiyear: passports, estate documents, long-term IDs, major property records.
- Event-based: birth, adoption, marriage, divorce, move, name change, new dependent, property purchase, major diagnosis, death, or material financial change.

## Recommended Resource Section

For the first version, include one personally vetted recommendation:

**Savor Folio Document Organizer**

Recommended as a physical home for protected household documents. If affiliate links are used later, disclose that clearly.

## Minimum Viable Paid Version

The first sellable version does not need deep guidance for all eight envelopes. It needs enough structure to be useful immediately:

- Cover
- How to use
- Master contents
- Identity Documents Checklist
- Important Documents Inventory
- Insurance Policy Register
- Emergency Information Sheet
- Digital Access Locator
- Continuity Instructions Starter
- Review Rhythm
- Disclaimer
