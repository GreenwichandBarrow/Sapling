# Pinterest Launch Assets

Draft only. Do not publish without Kay's approval.

## Channel Role

Pinterest is the initial discovery channel because it behaves more like visual search than personality-led social media. The account can be brand-led, not founder-led.

Pinterest is not expected to validate the paid product immediately by itself. It is a compounding discovery channel and should be paired with the owned site and, if approved, a near-term marketplace test.

## Pin Concepts

### Pin: Identity Documents Checklist

Headline: Identity Documents Checklist

Description: A minimalist checklist for organizing passports, birth certificates, Social Security cards, and other household identity records.

Destination: free checklist page.

### Pin: Important Documents File

Headline: TPFO Household Protection Binder

Description: A printable system for organizing important household documents, insurance details, access notes, and continuity instructions.

Destination: paid product page.

### Pin: What Belongs In A TPFO Household Protection Binder

Headline: What Belongs In A TPFO Household Protection Binder

Description: A clear starting point for organizing the documents and instructions that help a modern household stay current.

Destination: Risk & Protection page.

### Pin: Organize Identity Documents

Headline: Organize Identity Documents

Description: Separate documents you receive once from documents you need to renew or keep current.

Destination: Identity Documents page.

## Visual Direction

- Warm neutral paper.
- Charcoal serif headline.
- Soft peach linework.
- Quiet lock or safe icon.
- Cropped previews of the printable.
- No founder photo.
- No lifestyle stock photo dependency.
- No bright planner aesthetic.
