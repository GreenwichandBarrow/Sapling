# Platform And Acquisition Notes

Date checked: September 11, 2026

## Purpose

Support the first paid-product test with current platform facts, channel assumptions, and open approval items.

## Platform Facts

### Gumroad

Source: https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees

- Direct sales on Gumroad are listed as 10% + $0.50 plus payment processing.
- Gumroad Discover sales are listed as 30%, including processing.
- Gumroad states that it became merchant of record on January 1, 2025.

### Payhip

Source: https://payhip.com/pricing

- Free plan: $0/month plus 5% transaction fee.
- Plus plan: $29/month plus 2% transaction fee.
- Pro plan: $99/month and no Payhip transaction fee.
- PayPal or Stripe processing fees still apply.
- Payhip supports digital products.

### Stripe

Source: https://stripe.com/pricing

- Standard domestic card pricing is listed as 2.9% + $0.30 per successful transaction.
- Stripe is a payments layer, not a marketplace or discovery engine.

### Etsy

Sources:

- https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035902374-Etsy-Fee-Basics
- https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/

- Etsy charges a $0.20 listing fee.
- Etsy lists a 6.5% transaction fee.
- Etsy Payments processing fees vary by country; the United States is listed in Etsy's payments policy as 3% + $0.25.
- Etsy may have additional fees for optional tools such as Etsy Ads or Offsite Ads.

### Pinterest

Source: https://business.pinterest.com/audience/

- Pinterest reports 640 million monthly active users.
- Pinterest reports its global audience as 70% women.
- Pinterest reports that it reaches 40% of U.S. households with annual income over $150K.

## Launch Platform Interpretation

### Fastest Paid Test

**Hypothesis:** Gumroad or Payhip is the fastest way to sell and deliver a PDF without building custom commerce.

Reason:

- Both are built for digital delivery.
- Both avoid custom checkout implementation.
- Both can be linked from the owned site.

Tradeoff:

- Neither solves discovery by itself.
- Gumroad takes higher direct-sale fees but handles more tax complexity as merchant of record.
- Payhip has lower platform fees on the free plan, but Stripe or PayPal processing still applies.

### Fastest Search-Intent Test

**Hypothesis:** Etsy may be the fastest way to test buyer intent from strangers already searching for document binders and printable household systems.

Reason:

- Etsy has built-in marketplace search.
- Similar products already exist in the category.

Tradeoff:

- Etsy's marketplace language often skews toward emergency binders, just-in-case binders, and busy printable aesthetics.
- The Private Family Office should use Etsy as a test surface, not as the primary brand home.
- Listing fees and any ads require Kay approval.

### Long-Term Brand Channel

**Hypothesis:** Pinterest plus SEO is the best long-term fit because it can be brand-led, visual, and search-oriented without requiring Kay to become the public face.

Tradeoff:

- Pinterest and SEO are unlikely to validate paid demand over one weekend without an existing audience.
- They should still be started because they can compound.

## Recommended Weekend Choice

For the weekend test, prepare for two paths:

1. **Owned site + Gumroad or Payhip checkout** for brand-controlled sales and automatic delivery.
2. **Etsy listing draft** as an optional marketplace test if Kay approves the listing fee and marketplace positioning.

## Approval Items

- Whether to create an Etsy listing.

- Whether to run any paid Pinterest or Etsy ads.
- Whether search-agreement or conflict review is needed before accepting payments.
