# Field Guide

Risk & Protection is the part of a private family office that keeps important household information clear, protected, and usable. In a modern home, that information lives in more than one place. Some records are digital. Some originals still matter physically. Some information is held by professionals, institutions, schools, insurers, or government agencies.

The goal is not to gather every document into one paper binder. The goal is to build a system that lets the right person understand what exists, where it lives, what needs to be current, and how to find it when needed.

A useful household protection system has four parts:

- **Document control:** a map of the important documents and records the household should be able to locate.
- **Digital order:** a clear folder structure and file-naming convention so records are findable.
- **Protected originals:** a safe place for the original documents that still need physical protection.
- **Review rhythm:** a simple schedule for keeping the system current as documents expire, policies renew, children grow, family circumstances change, or new records are created.

Start with identity documents because they are concrete and easy to recognize. From there, expand into insurance, legal and estate documents, emergency information, property records, account access, and continuity instructions.
