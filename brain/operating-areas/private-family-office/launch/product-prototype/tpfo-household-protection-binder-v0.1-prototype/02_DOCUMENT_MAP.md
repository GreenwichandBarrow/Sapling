# Document Map

This map is the reference list for the first version of the Household Protection Binder. It is intentionally broader than the free Identity Documents Checklist.

## Identity Documents

- Birth certificates
- Social Security cards
- Passports
- Driver licenses
- State IDs
- Marriage certificates
- Divorce decrees
- Adoption records
- Citizenship or naturalization records
- Immigration documents
- School identity forms
- Travel consent forms

## Insurance Policies

- Homeowners or renters insurance
- Auto insurance
- Umbrella insurance
- Life insurance
- Disability insurance
- Health insurance
- Dental and vision insurance
- Long-term care insurance if applicable
- Valuable articles or collections policies
- Pet insurance if applicable

## Legal & Estate Documents

- Wills
- Trust documents
- Powers of attorney
- Health care directives
- Guardianship documents
- Prenuptial or postnuptial agreements if applicable
- Property deeds
- Vehicle titles
- Business ownership records if applicable

## Emergency Information

- Emergency contacts
- Doctors and preferred hospital
- Allergies and critical medical notes
- Current medications list location
- School and childcare contacts
- Pet care contacts
- Property access notes

## Digital Access

- Password manager location
- Recovery email accounts
- Device passcode access plan
- Cloud storage location
- Important subscription accounts
- Two-factor authentication method
- Digital legacy settings where applicable

## Property & Asset Records

- Home purchase documents
- Mortgage or lease documents
- Property tax records
- Home improvement records
- Warranties
- Appraisals
- Jewelry, art, and collection records
- Safe deposit box details if applicable

## Beneficiaries & Account Access

- Retirement accounts
- Life insurance policies
- Bank or brokerage accounts
- Transfer-on-death or payable-on-death designations
- Education savings accounts
- Health savings accounts

## Continuity Instructions

- Recurring bills
- Household vendors
- School-year obligations
- Care routines
- Insurance contacts
- Legal or tax professionals
- Where key documents live
- What should be reviewed first
