# Digital Folder System

Create this structure in the digital storage system you already use: Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive, or a secure local drive.

## Recommended Folder Structure

TPFO Household Protection Binder

- 01 Identity Documents
- 02 Insurance Policies
- 03 Legal Estate Documents
- 04 Emergency Information
- 05 Digital Access Locator
- 06 Property Asset Records
- 07 Beneficiaries Account Access
- 08 Continuity Instructions
- 09 Review Rhythm
- 10 Archive

## Setup Guidance

Keep the structure simple enough that another responsible person could understand it without explanation.

Use the folders for copies, scans, confirmation records, and notes about where originals live. Do not use them to store sensitive information insecurely.

For highly sensitive files, use the storage protections available in your system: strong passwords, two-factor authentication, limited sharing, encrypted storage where appropriate, and professional advice when needed.
