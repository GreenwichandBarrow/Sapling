# Protected Originals Guide

Some household documents still matter as physical originals. The digital system should tell you where they live, but the originals themselves should be stored in a protected place.

## Usually Good Candidates For Protected Physical Storage

- Birth certificates
- Social Security cards
- Passports when not traveling
- Marriage certificates
- Divorce decrees
- Adoption records
- Citizenship or naturalization records
- Immigration documents
- Original estate documents if your attorney has advised you to keep them
- Property deeds or vehicle titles if originals are held at home
- Insurance declaration pages if you keep a physical household file

## Usually Not Stored As Originals In A Household Organizer

- Driver license used day to day
- Current health insurance card used day to day
- Payment cards
- Passwords, PINs, or full account credentials
