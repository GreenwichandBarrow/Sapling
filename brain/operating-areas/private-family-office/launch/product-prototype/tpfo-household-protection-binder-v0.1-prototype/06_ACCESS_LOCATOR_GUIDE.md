# Access Locator Guide

The access locator is not a password list. It is a map of where access is managed and who should know how to locate it.

## What To Record

- Account or system name
- Category
- Where access is managed
- Recovery email or phone if appropriate
- Who should know how to locate access
- Review timing
- Notes that do not reveal credentials

## Examples

- Password manager: Apple Passwords, 1Password, Bitwarden, or another secure manager
- Cloud storage: Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, OneDrive
- Device access: where instructions are held, not the passcode itself
- Two-factor authentication: where the method lives, not recovery codes in plain text

## Privacy Rule

Do not write passwords, PINs, Social Security numbers, full account numbers, or recovery codes into an unsecured spreadsheet or printable.
