# First 60-Minute Setup

This sequence is designed to help the buyer start without turning the project into a full weekend of administration.

## First 10 Minutes

Create the main digital folder and the eight core subfolders.

## Next 15 Minutes

Open the Document Inventory workbook. Add only the documents you already know exist. Do not search the whole house yet.

## Next 15 Minutes

Choose one person in the household and complete the Identity Documents section for that person.

## Next 10 Minutes

Identify which originals should be in protected physical storage. Put a note in the workbook if you know where they are.

## Final 10 Minutes

Set the next review date. If you cannot finish today, record the next small action rather than leaving the system open-ended.
