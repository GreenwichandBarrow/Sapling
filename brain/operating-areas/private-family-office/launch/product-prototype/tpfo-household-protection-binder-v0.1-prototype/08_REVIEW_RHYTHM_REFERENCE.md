# Review Rhythm Reference

The review rhythm keeps the system current.

## Monthly

Use monthly review only for active changes: newly received documents, recent moves, open claims, new school forms, or records that arrived and need to be filed.

## Quarterly

Use quarterly review for open administrative loops: pending renewals, in-progress legal updates, unresolved insurance claims, major vendor changes, or documents still missing from the inventory.

## Annually

Use annual review for insurance policies, beneficiary confirmations, school forms, tax document storage, household contacts, and continuity instructions.

## Multiyear

Use multiyear review for passports, driver licenses, state IDs, immigration documents, estate documents, property records, and other records that renew or require attention on a longer cycle.

## Event-Based

Review after birth, adoption, marriage, divorce, death, move, name change, new dependent, property purchase, major diagnosis, material financial change, or meaningful household responsibility change.
