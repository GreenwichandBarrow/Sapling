# Product Scope Notes For Kay

This prototype is meant to answer one question: does the product feel substantive enough to become a $49 launch test?

## What Feels Stronger Than A Printable Binder

- It gives the buyer a digital structure to create.
- It gives them a workbook to manage the system over time.
- It keeps physical storage focused only on protected originals.
- It includes review rhythm, which can later become an email reminder or membership feature.
- It avoids asking the buyer to write sensitive information into an unsafe document.

## What Still Needs Judgment

- Whether the product should include Excel only, Google Sheets only, or both.
- Whether the folder structure should be delivered as instructions or as a downloadable ZIP folder template.
- Whether the first version should include a fillable PDF at all.
- Whether $49 is still the right first price after reviewing the prototype.
