# Weekend Launch Plan

Date: September 11, 2026

## Objective

Launch a small, credible paid-product test for The Private Family Office: one useful digital product that a stranger can understand, purchase, and receive automatically.

This is an income test, not proof of salary replacement. The goal is to collect evidence: visits, email signups, paid purchases, refunds, and buyer questions.

## Reusable Work

- Brand positioning: **A private family office system for the modern home.**
- Homepage visual direction: eight flat books, 4 across by 2 rows on desktop, grey and peach neutral palette.
- First live book: **Risk & Protection**.
- First envelope structure: confidential tie-envelope interface with a reading panel.
- First free sample: **Identity Documents Checklist**.
- First paid-product direction: **Important Documents / Household Protection File**.
- Initial discovery channel: Pinterest as visual search, supported by SEO on the owned site.
- Product recommendation standard: recommend only products Kay has personally used, tested, or directly vetted.
- Personally vetted product: Savor Folio Document Organizer.

## Customer Acquisition Test

The near-term problem is not just production. The test needs traffic from people who are already searching for a solution.

### Owned Site + Pinterest

Pinterest fits the brand because it acts more like visual search than personality-led social media. It is slower than a marketplace but can compound over time.

Initial Pinterest asset themes:

- Important Documents Checklist
- Household Protection File
- Family Office System for the Home
- Identity Documents Checklist
- Organizing Important Documents
- Home Document Organizer
- Modern Household Binder

### Etsy Marketplace Test

Etsy is not the long-term brand home, but it may provide faster search-intent exposure for terms such as emergency binder, important documents binder, family documents organizer, and just-in-case binder.

The brand risk is that Etsy's visual environment is busy and often crisis-framed. The test listing should keep Private Family Office language calm and differentiated while using marketplace search terms where necessary.

Potential test cost: listing fees and platform transaction/payment fees. Kay must approve before listing.
