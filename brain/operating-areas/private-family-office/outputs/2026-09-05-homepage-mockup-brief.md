# Homepage Mockup Brief

## Objective

Create a first static homepage mockup for The Private Family Office that tests the core navigation mechanic: eight flat interactive books, arranged as a calm reference set for women becoming heads of household.

The mockup should answer one question: does this feel like a serious, self-serve household operating library worth entering?

## Audience

Women becoming heads of household, especially women carrying both breadwinner responsibility and the operational burden of children, home, documents, risk, finances, health, travel, time, and giving.

## Facts

- The venture is separate from Greenwich & Barrow, Kay's search fund, Chief of Staff work, and Kay's personal family-office planning.
- The product is a self-serve digital library, not consulting, custom setup, or client service.
- The homepage should show eight books, not shelves.
- Desktop layout should be 4 books across and 2 rows.
- Each book opens into one household domain.
- The visual language should be minimalist, organic, quiet, academically grounded, and zen-neutral.
- The palette should use variations of grey and peach, with different darkness, warmth, and brightness.

## Assumptions

- A finite set of eight books will make the household-management burden feel legible and contained.
- The audience needs orientation before optimization.
- The homepage should behave more like a reference library entrance than a blog, course landing page, or founder-led brand page.
- The working book titles are useful enough for the first mockup, even if the final names change.

## Hypotheses

- The eight-book grid can become the core brand/navigation mechanic.
- Flat books will feel more restrained and credible than cards, tiles, icons, or a literal bookshelf.
- Academic, reference-library language will differentiate the site from mommy blogs, luxury lifestyle content, productivity influencers, and financial-advice brands.

## Layout

Desktop:

- Minimal top area with small brand placeholder: "The Private Family Office".
- One restrained positioning sentence near the top: "A private family office system for the modern home."
- Eight flat books in a 4 x 2 grid.
- Each book should be large enough to read but not oversized like a marketing hero card.
- The grid should feel like a quiet desk or archive surface, not a retail product wall.

Mobile:

- Books can collapse to 2 across or 1 across depending on legibility.
- Titles must remain readable without cramped line breaks.
- The page should remain calm and uncluttered.

## Book Covers

Each book cover should include:

- Working title
- Optional small volume number, e.g. "Volume I"
- Optional short subtitle if needed for clarity

Working titles:

1. Household Finance
2. Risk & Protection
3. Home Operations
4. Children & Dependents
5. Health & Environment
6. Travel & Mobility
7. Time & Calendar
8. Giving & Community

## Visual Style

Use:

- Warm greys
- Soft stone
- Muted peach
- Clay-peach
- Pale taupe
- Off-white background
- Fine serif or academic-feeling title treatment paired with a quiet sans-serif interface font
- Subtle shadows or outlines only if needed to distinguish the books
- Generous spacing

Avoid:

- Decorative florals
- Script fonts
- Pink-forward femininity
- Influencer templates
- Luxury gold/black palette
- Loud gradients
- Blog-card layouts
- Founder portrait
- Social proof blocks
- Newsletter-first pressure
- Course-sales language

## Interaction

In the mockup, each book should appear clickable. The interaction can be represented visually with hover/open states later, but the first mockup only needs to show the main closed-book homepage.

Future interaction direction:

- Click or tap opens a book.
- Inside each book are five sections: Field Guide, Checklist, Product Recommendations, Digital Products, People Recommendations.

## Copy Hierarchy

Keep copy minimal.

Primary line:

**The Private Family Office**

Positioning line:

**A private family office system for the modern home.**

Optional supporting line:

**A quiet reference library for women becoming heads of household.**

Avoid long explanatory homepage copy in the first mockup.

## Exclusions

Do not include:

- About Kay
- Founder story
- Consulting CTA
- Book a call
- Community CTA
- Social links as a primary action
- AI positioning
- G&B language
- Search fund language
- Personal family-office planning language

## Open Questions

- Should the top brand read "The Private Family Office" or a placeholder until naming is resolved?
- Should books use volume numbers or just titles?
- Should subtitles appear on the covers, or only inside the opened book?
- Should desktop books appear as perfect rectangles or slightly book-like with spine/depth cues?
- Should the first mockup show hover/open behavior or only the closed-book grid?
