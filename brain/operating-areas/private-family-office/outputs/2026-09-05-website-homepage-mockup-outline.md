# Website Homepage Mockup Outline

## Objective

Test whether the homepage feels like a serious, self-serve household operating library for women becoming heads of household.

## Core Visual

Eight flat books.

No shelf. No cards. No blog grid.

On desktop:

- 4 books across
- 2 rows

## Brand Line

**The Private Family Office**

*A private family office system for the modern home.*

## Optional Supporting Line

*A quiet reference library for women becoming heads of household.*

## Book Titles

1. Household Finance
2. Risk & Protection
3. Home Operations
4. Children & Dependents
5. Health & Environment
6. Travel & Mobility
7. Time & Calendar
8. Giving & Community

## Inside Each Book

Each book opens into:

- Field Guide
- Checklist
- Product Recommendations
- Digital Products
- People Recommendations

## Visual Style

Organic minimalism. Zen neutrals. Quiet, academic, useful.

Use grey and peach variations, not a loud palette.

The books should feel like a calm reference set, not decorative lifestyle objects.

## Avoid

- Founder story
- Kay as public face
- Consulting CTA
- "Book a call"
- Social-media-forward design
- Mommy-blog cues
- Influencer tone
- Luxury gold/black
- Cute illustrations
- AI positioning
- Greenwich & Barrow references
- Search fund references

## Design Choices to Test

- Whether the book titles feel too institutional
- Whether the 4x2 grid has enough visual warmth
- Whether the page feels useful rather than empty
- Whether books need volume numbers
- Whether covers need subtitles or should stay spare
