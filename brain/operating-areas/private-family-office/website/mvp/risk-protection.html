Identity Documents

Identity documents are the records that establish who each person is and how the household is legally connected. They should have a clear physical home, and the right person should know where to find them. Some documents rarely change, but others need attention when they expire, when a child is born, when someone changes names, when a family moves, or when school, travel, or legal requirements change.
