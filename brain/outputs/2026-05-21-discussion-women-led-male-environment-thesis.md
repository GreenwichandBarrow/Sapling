# Discussion — Women-led thesis sharpened to male-dominated environment qualifier

**Direction chosen:** You are committing to acquire, or fund the acquisition of, women-led businesses that operate inside male-dominated industries. The industry environment around the business must be predominantly male, whether in working population, leadership composition, or both. The leader must be a woman. Female-dominated industries are excluded, even when the leader is a woman, because the working environment is the wrong one for you.

Yesterday's [[outputs/2026-05-20-discussion-women-led-thesis-convergence|convergence brief]] named the women-empowerment throughline as your career's organizing principle (fashion → Chanel → Myself Renewed → search → post-exit female investor). The realization was real, but you could not yet articulate it in a form that worked for yourself or for [[entities/sarah-rowell|Sarah Rowell]] (your WSN mentor) whom you meet today. 6. **Final form: women-led businesses inside male-dominated industries** — accepted, pressure-tested, and confirmed.
