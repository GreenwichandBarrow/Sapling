# Pest 10-Co June Experiment — Head-to-Head vs DealsX + JJ

A two-track, four-week experiment to test whether the [[memory/feedback-industry-is-output-of-network|network-mapping-first methodology]] outperforms the active DealsX + JJ volume channels on Premium Pest Management, the Bucket-1 niche under the [[memory/user-kay-women-led-purpose-throughline|women-led purpose throughline]]. The verdict drops June 30. If the 10-company effort beats DealsX + JJ on the metrics below, both volume channels wind down on the schedule already noticed. If it does not, both stay running and the methodology is reassessed.

---

## A. Goal — What "Outperform" Means

The 10-co effort outperforms in June if, by 2026-06-30 end-of-day, it has produced **at least one of the following two outcomes** AND a positive top-of-funnel signal that beats the benchmark rates from [[brain/outputs/2026-05-26-dealsx-vs-jj-performance-analysis|the channel-performance analysis]]:

1. **≥1 meaningful conversation** (defined: live owner conversation with two-way exchange beyond pleasantries — phone, video, or in-person — with intent captured in a Granola note or Attio note), OR
2. **≥1 NDA executed** sourced from the 10-co list.

Plus the top-of-funnel floor:

3. **Positive-reply rate ≥ 30% of recipients** (3 of 10) — vs. DealsX's 0.94% positive-of-sent. This is the rate that justifies the per-firm research overhead relative to volume cold email. Below this rate the experiment failed at top-of-funnel and the rest of the metric stack is moot.

Weighting (highest to lowest):
- NDA = decisive single signal (3x weight)
- Meaningful conversation = decisive single signal (2x weight)
- Positive reply rate = floor signal (1x weight)

A single NDA from the 10-co list **alone** wins the experiment regardless of reply rate. A 30%+ positive-reply rate **alone** does not — it must produce at least one meaningful conversation or NDA to flip the verdict.

## C. Per-Firm Research Depth (Pre-Outreach Deliverable)

Before any outreach, each of the 10 firms gets a one-paragraph research dossier covering:

- **Owner name** (verified via firm About page, LinkedIn, or M/WBE certification record)
- **Owner backstory** — particularly if women-led: how she came into the business (founder, second-gen, married into, acquired), years operating, professional background
- **Firm size signals** — only what is disclosed publicly (employee count from LinkedIn, fleet size from About page, geographic footprint, "since 19XX" anchor). No revenue estimates and no Apollo enrichment in this experiment — that is the volume-channel input.
- **Conviction-builder signals** — commercial mix, IPM/HACCP focus, MWBE certification, named clients, association involvement (PWIPM, NPMA Women's Forum, NY state chapter), philanthropy, any women-in-business award.
- **Warm-intro path** — explicit pathway (Allison Allen, Leigh Fryxell, PWIPM Council member, NY pest chapter contact, prior conference touchpoint). If none yet exists, state "cold — needs warm-intro mapping before send" — this is the trigger to ask the Forum coordinator OR widen the search through Sarah Rowell (WSN), Megan Lawlor, or other female network anchors.

Output format: one markdown file at `brain/outputs/2026-06-{date}-pest-10-co-research-dossier.md` with one paragraph per firm. Deliverable due before the first send — first week of June.

## F. Decision Criteria — June 30 Verdict

**The 10-co effort wins (DealsX + JJ wind down on the existing notice timeline) if either condition holds:**

- **Condition 1:** ≥1 NDA executed from the 10-co list, OR
- **Condition 2:** ≥1 meaningful owner conversation **AND** ≥3 positive replies (30% positive-reply rate)

**The 10-co effort loses (DealsX + JJ both stay running) if:**

- 0 NDAs **AND** 0 meaningful conversations, regardless of reply rate, OR
- Fewer than 3 positive replies AND fewer than 1 meaningful conversation (i.e., methodology didn't even clear the noise floor)

**The 10-co effort is inconclusive (run a 30-day extension, both channels continue) if:**

- ≥3 positive replies but 0 meaningful conversations and 0 NDAs — top-of-funnel works, middle-of-funnel pace was too slow. Extend to July 31 with the same list before verdict.

The thresholds above are deliberately asymmetric: it takes ONE strong outcome (NDA or qualified conversation) to win, but TWO failures (no NDA AND no conversation) to lose. This protects against the small-sample noise that a 10-firm list carries.
