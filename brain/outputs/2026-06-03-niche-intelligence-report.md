## Target TAM (informational — not scored)

- **US firms:** highly fragmented; ~90% of the market is small regional/local independents (most <500 communities). ~365,000 community associations nationally.
- **PE-backed/acquired:** growing platforms (RealManage/American Securities, Continuum/CIVC, Oakline/Alpine, Pioneer/FFL) but still a small share of firm count.
- **PE consolidation risk:** Medium and rising — concentrated on firms >$1M EBITDA (bid to ~8-10x). Sub-$1M owner-run firms remain below PE radar (~2-4x off-market).
- **Long Island / NYC metro target pool:** 100+ small independents. ## Key Risks & Diligence Landmines

- **Cancellable contracts (THE risk):** management agreements often terminable on 30-60 days notice; revenue can walk. Verify term, notice period, auto-renewal, and recent termination notices.
- **Key-employee dependence:** community managers, not the owner, hold board relationships. Lock retention pre-close.
- **Trust/operating-account controls:** firms hold association funds — fraud/liability fault line. Bring a CPA; audit segregation, signatories, reconciliations.
- **Margin at sub-scale:** below ~500 doors, margins are thin; underwrite the path to scale.
- **PE competition / refragmentation:** the G&B size band ($1.5-5M EBITDA) overlaps where PE shops. Edge = off-market sourcing via Guillermo + CAI, not banker auctions.
- **NY specifics:** HOAs/condos/co-ops governed by NYS Not-For-Profit Corporation Law (buy the for-profit MANAGER, not the association); NYC co-op boards are notoriously demanding; Long Island has dense, accessible HOA/condo/55+ communities.
