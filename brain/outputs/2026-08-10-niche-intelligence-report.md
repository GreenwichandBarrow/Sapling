Umbrella theme: Trust, Compliance & Verification / property-compliance infrastructure.

Growth trend: urban flooding, water-quality enforcement, MS4/NPDES maintenance obligations, HOA reserve and maintenance accountability, aging stormwater infrastructure, and green-infrastructure adoption.

Operational complexity created: property owners and boards must keep detention ponds, underground systems, bioswales, drains, and BMP/SCM assets inspected, functional, maintained, documented, and violation-free across recurring monthly, quarterly, and annual cycles.

Why it is an edge niche: this is not property management, HOA management, landscaping, engineering design, or construction. It is the recurring vendor that inspects, cleans, documents, repairs, and coordinates compliance for installed stormwater assets.
