# Decision Trace: Bias Toward YES on Personal Introductions, EVALUATE on Deal Pitches

## Context

`/socrates` session 2026-05-20 — pushback from Kay on a pattern of prior Claude sessions over-applying "decline what does not fit." Kay raised a specific prior incident: a previous session advised her to decline two startup introductions (push to future date) because startups were not her thesis. She sent that message. The contact never replied again. Relationship lost. Kay said: *"I'm also a little hesitant to take your advice to not pursue something or not take a phone call or not meet an owner. I think my bias should be towards the introduction."* Then later, anchored in her own pre-existing wisdom from her independent-sponsor neighbor: *"never turn down a conversation because you never know what can come of it."*

## Decisions

### Splitting one decision rule into two
**AI proposed (prior framing):** uniform "evaluate against fit gates, decline if fails" — applied to specific deal pitches AND personal introductions identically.
**Chosen this session:** TWO different decision rules.
- **Personal introduction (new person, new network node):** default-accept. The intro IS the asset, even if the introducing person's pipeline does not fit Kay's thesis. Future-deal value from the relationship is the upside; the meeting cost is small. Use soft time-frame ask if timing is bad ("can we schedule for X weeks out"). Do NOT decline. Declining signals "I am not in the relationship business with you" and kills the source.
- **Specific deal pitch from a known source:** evaluate against fit gates. Decline cleanly if it fails the gates, but gift calibration: tell them what TO send next time. This is honest deal-flow education for the source AND preserves the relationship.
**Reasoning:** the previous uniform rule cost Kay a contact she valued. The conflation was hiding the actual decision being made. A future agent applying the new rule must classify inbound (intro vs deal pitch) BEFORE drafting any decline. This becomes a primary classification step in the AI drafting skill spec.
**Pattern:** #pattern/intro-vs-deal-decision-rules #pattern/relationship-preservation

### What "intro" means specifically
**AI proposed:** undefined — prior sessions treated all "thoughtful response under uncertainty" the same way.
**Chosen this session:** an introduction is anyone offering a new person or new network node. Even if Kay does not see immediate deal-flow value. Even if the introducing person's pipeline is misaligned (e.g., the Cell-side advisor who sends digital-business deals — he is still a relationship asset and intro source, just reclassified from primary pipeline). Even if the timing for a call is bad — accept the intro, propose a later time.
**Reasoning:** Kay's edge is relational; her sourcing engine is network density. Each new person is potentially the warm-intro path to a target three steps away. The negative control: declining the two startup intros made the contact go silent. The positive control: her independent-sponsor neighbor's rule, which Kay has applied successfully before.
**Pattern:** #pattern/intro-vs-deal-decision-rules

## Learnings

- **For AI drafting skill spec:** classification step must run BEFORE drafting. Routes: (a) personal intro → default-accept template; (b) deal pitch → fit-gate-evaluate template; (c) warm-network check-in → cadence template; (d) advisor counsel request → strategic-response template.
- **For pipeline-manager and email-intelligence:** when flagging an inbound for Kay's response queue, label by type. Intros never appear as "consider declining"; only as "schedule it" or "propose later if timing is bad."
- **For investor / advisor relationship recommendations:** reclassify advisors whose pipeline rarely fits (e.g., Cell-side digital-focus) from "decline or pursue" to "always meet, always warm" — they are network nodes, not deal sources.
- **For future Claude sessions:** if you find yourself recommending "decline this intro because the pipeline does not fit," stop. The intro is not the pipeline. Recommend "accept the meeting, propose a time that works."
- **Override / human override flag:** Kay corrected the prior-session pattern explicitly. This is a HUMAN OVERRIDE trace — agent default was wrong; Kay's rule is the right one going forward.
