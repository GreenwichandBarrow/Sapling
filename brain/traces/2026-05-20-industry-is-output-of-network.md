# Decision Trace: Industry is the OUTPUT of Network, Not the INPUT

## Context

`/socrates` session 2026-05-20 — Kay had been spending 15 months selecting industries top-down: research, get investor buy-in, then hunt for targets within. Three industries (software, insurance, pest) had cycled in and out. Investor pushback ("picked over", "competitive", "no background") had killed two. The same pattern: pick an industry on financial/sector criteria, struggle to source, get pushback, drop, restart. Today she also rediscovered her own post-MBA job-search methodology (which landed Chanel): build a list of 10 names across 3 areas (PE, Mgmt Consulting, Luxury Strategy), figure out connections, coffee with as many people as possible. She INTENDED to use that method for the search and drifted into the conventional national-volume playbook. Sam at the mid-search summit this week independently validated the same shape: "5 companies, do everything to get the call."

## Decisions

### Inverting industry-selection direction
**AI proposed (prior sessions, conventional search-fund logic):** thesis-driven sector selection first, then target-discovery within the chosen sector, then warm-intro and outreach to specific targets within.
**Chosen this session:** for Kay specifically, industry is the OUTPUT of network access, not the INPUT. Pick which female-led networks she can plug into in NY, let the industries those networks sit in be the thesis output. Top-down sector selection is a national-volume-search-fund playbook that does not match how she has ever won — and is partly why 15 months have produced no targets in her chosen industries.
**Reasoning:** Kay's lock-in mechanism is relational, not analytical. The industries that stuck (Chanel, pest with women's association, insurance via a woman) were entered through a person/network, not through research. The industries that dropped (software, art storage when it turned out male-dominated, insurance after the discouraging large-strategic call) had the relational anchor removed. Her own pre-MBA method (3 buckets x 10 names + network triangulation + coffee) was the embodiment of this — she just forgot it for 15 months while trying to fit conventional thesis structure.
**Pattern:** #pattern/network-driven-thesis #pattern/methodology-rediscovery

### What this changes for /plan and target-discovery
**AI proposed (prior plan structures):** target-discovery follows thesis approval; thesis approval depends on financial-fit screen.
**Chosen this session:** for Kay's planning, the operational shape is "3 buckets of 10 women-owned NY firms each" (Chanel-era methodology + Sam's "tight list" validation + Harrison Wells custom-send approach). Industry comes from the bucket (pest from women's pest network, two more buckets from adjacent staple-female-skew industries WHERE female-led networks already exist). Investor pitch frames this as "structured thesis with warm-intro optionality," not opportunism.
**Reasoning:** future agents sequencing plan-mode or target-discovery for Kay should default to network-first, industry-as-derivative. The convergence brief at `[[outputs/2026-05-20-discussion-women-led-thesis-convergence]]` is the input to /plan.
**Pattern:** #pattern/network-driven-thesis

## Learnings

- **For target-discovery skill calls:** prefer network-mapping-first when generating new candidate firms for Kay. Apollo or list-builder pulls should be filtered by "does a female-led association/network exist in this industry that Kay can plug into" before generating the list.
- **For thesis-coherence checks (CIO agent):** evaluate Kay's thesis cohesion on the network-access axis, not the conventional sector-depth axis. Cross-industry warm-intro optionality is structurally honest given her access pattern, not opportunism.
- **For investor-update framing:** position regional concentration as "sourcing concentration in NY because that is where my edge produces highest-conviction conversations" — a sourcing-logic frame, not a geographic restriction.
- **For partner / hire recommendations:** any hire must support the network-driven engine, not replicate national-volume infrastructure. DealsX and JJ being wound down by summer (decision this session) is consistent with this.
- **Negative-pattern flag:** if a future agent finds itself listing 1000-target buy-box-screen lists for Kay, that is the drift. Pull back to 3 buckets of 10 each.
