# Decision Trace: Women-Led Thesis Requires the Male-Dominated-Environment Qualifier

## Context
The 2026-05-20 /socrates session named "supporting women in business" as Kay's load-bearing organizing principle. A 2026-05-21 continuation tried to compress it into a shareable purpose for her WSN mentor (Sarah Rowell) and a forward operating rule. The naive form — "acquire women-led / women-owned businesses" — kept feeling incomplete to Kay.

## Decisions

### What the organizing principle actually is
**AI initial framing:** women-owned businesses (the 5/20 capture), with art storage logged as "interest collapsed when industry turned out male-dominated."
**Chosen (Kay's correction):** **women-led businesses IN MALE-DOMINATED industries.** Two required conditions — the leader is a woman AND the surrounding industry environment is predominantly male. Female-dominated industries are excluded even when the leader is a woman.
**Reasoning:** Kay surfaced a "weird dichotomy" — she wants to support women but does *not* want to work surrounded by women day-to-day. Probing it resolved the apparent contradiction: she has actively *chosen* male-dominated environments her whole life (science competitions, enrichment programs, fashion's male strategy floor, Chanel's male CEO/real-estate side). She walked away from women's health *because* it was female-dominated. Art storage interest is genuine and persists — the male-dominated field is the draw, which directly contradicts the 5/20 capture (corrected here).
**Pattern:** #pattern/women-led-in-male-dominated-industries

### How industries get selected under the sharpened rule
**AI initial claim:** "industries fall out of network mapping" (industries skipped).
**Chosen (Kay caught it):** industries are still selected **up front**, but by a *different rule* — male-dominated environment + female-led network presence + NY access + travel-light — replacing the traditional TAM/buy-box screen. High-touch outreach needs named targets → needs buckets → needs industries, so industries cannot be skipped. The thesis is *downstream* of the organizing principle.

## Alternatives Considered
- **"Women-led businesses" alone** — rejected: validates female-dominated targets (women's health, women-led marketing agency) that Kay actively rejects.
- **Pick women OR hang-with-men identity** — rejected: false dichotomy; the two coexist by design (credibility in male arenas + capital directed to the women who chose them).
- **Marketing/positioning first, then operations** — rejected: positioning should reflect evidence surfaced during the work, not a hypothesis written ahead of it.

Without the male-dominated qualifier, a future agent will surface female-dominated niches as on-thesis whenever a woman leads them — the exact error the 5/20 memory encoded (art storage backwards). The qualifier is the load-bearing filter, and it inverts several prior interpretations.
