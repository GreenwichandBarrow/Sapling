# Investor edge + network don't override economics

## Trigger
HOA / community-association management surfaced with three strong signals at once: co-lead [[entities/guillermo-lavergne|Guillermo Lavergne]] ([[entities/ashford-ventures|Ashford]]) is an HOA domain expert, property management was already named in the PPM, and a women-led network (CAI Long Island chapter, woman-presided) was confirmed. It looked like a lock.

## Decision
Did NOT treat the investor/network edge as a reason to advance HOA (or its cousins property management / facilities). The family fails the binding gates: thin sub-scale margins + PE-elevated multiples (8-10x for $1M+ EBITDA firms) that break the 35% IRR.

## Alternatives Considered
- (a) Advance HOA on the strength of right-to-win (domain-expert co-lead + women network).
- (b) Hold/decline on the economics regardless of the edge.

## Reasoning
The edge helps SOURCE a deal — off-market access, certainty of close, legacy fit. It does not change the entry multiple a sophisticated seller expects, nor the structural margin of the business. A warm relationship gets the conversation; it does not buy a friends-and-family discount when the seller has an 8-10x PE comp down the street. Right-to-win is a sourcing advantage, not a substitute for economics.

## Why This Trace Matters
The industry scorecard and future agents will be tempted to upgrade a niche because of a strong investor or network fit. That fit belongs in sourcing and tie-breaking, not in the go/no-go economics screen — otherwise a structurally-walled niche gets a false green light.

## Key Insight
Score the industry economics (margins + multiples) first; let right-to-win break ties, never override gates.
