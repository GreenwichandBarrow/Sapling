# Decision Trace: Investor Update Leads With "Engine Built, Edge Identified," Not "Closer to a Deal"

## Context
Kay's Q1 2026 quarterly investor update was overdue (no Q4 2025 update sent — a deliberate pause to reflect on the prior year and build out the AI engine). She came to /socrates anxious about the six-month gap, wanting to think through content, not draft. We worked the headline before any deck work.

## Decisions

### What conviction the update should lead with
**AI proposed (first pass framing test):** Surfaced two candidate headlines — (1) "closer to closing a deal" vs (2) "the engine is built and the edge is identified."
**Chosen:** Lead with structural conviction — "engine built, edge identified." The one live opportunistic deal becomes *evidence of inventory*, not the headline.
**Reasoning:**
- The 2-deal proof base collapsed to 1 in real time: the ag-tech deal is dead (main investor vetoed on Colombia geography — bulk of business offshore). A "closer to a deal" headline built on a single fragile candidate recreates the exact over-promise/under-deliver trap (promise it, miss, owe another explanation in 90 days).
- Per [[feedback_court_case_psychology_identity_layer]], Kay's identity-to-performance wiring turns each update into "courtroom evidence" → avoidance. A headline keyed on an uncontrollable (will a specific deal close?) feeds the trap. A headline keyed on controllables (engine built, field cadence hit, edge identified) is defensible regardless of deal outcome.
- Under-promise on what she can't control, over-deliver on what she can.
**Pattern:** #pattern/structural-over-transactional

### How to handle the six-month gap
**Chosen:** Do not name it. No apology, no "I should have sent sooner," no Q4-as-defense. Q4 = deliberate reflection (cohort interviews with active searchers, recent acquirers, and those who didn't acquire) + AI build; Q1 = field execution. The dates on the deck do the work.
**Reasoning:** Reframing Q4 as agency (deliberate pause) rather than omission is the counter-move to the courtroom pattern. Also factually, 7 of 12 investors (Jeff, Guillermo, Will, Clayton, Tom, Doug, Luka) are current via 1:1 touch — the "silence" is with ~5, an operational cover-note/call question, not the headline.

## Learnings
- For Kay's investor updates specifically, **lead with engine/edge (controllables), frame any specific deal as by-product not headline.** A single live deal is too fragile to anchor a quarter's narrative — vetoes surface late and deal-specific (Colombia here).
- New durable rule captured in [[feedback_investor_update_lead_with_engine_not_deal]].
- START-bullet spinoff worth keeping in the deck: **pre-vet deals against investor hard constraints (geography, sector, structure) before deep diligence** — the Colombia veto is the fresh Q1 evidence for this.
- Candidate for `evolve` into investor-update skill (quarterly mode): default the headline to structural progress, not deal-proximity.
