## Context
Kay requested niche-intelligence processing for four related luxury/fragrance/packaging lanes: value-added fragrance distributors, high-end packaging, luxury package testing, and fragrance testing. The workflow created one-pagers and scorecards, uploaded them to Google Drive, and appended tracker rows to the Industry Research Tracker WEEKLY REVIEW tab.

## Decisions
### Treat Related Lanes As Four Separate Tracker Rows
**AI proposed:** Process the requested items through the niche-intelligence path with one-pager, scorecard, and tracker update.
**Chosen:** Created four separate rows: Value-Added Fragrance Distribution; High-End Beauty & Fragrance Packaging; Luxury Package Testing & Validation Labs; Fragrance & Cosmetic Product Testing Labs.
**Reasoning:** The economics and search fit differ materially: package/product testing are service/lab workflows, while distribution and packaging supply have more working-capital and product-supplier risk.
**Pattern:** #pattern/separate-related-niches-when-economics-differ

### Score Testing Lanes Above Distribution/Supply Lanes
**AI proposed:** Score each lane based on initial screen, A2LA target-density proxy, revenue quality, and Kay/search-fund fit.
**Chosen:** Scores: Value-Added Fragrance Distribution 2.05; High-End Beauty & Fragrance Packaging 2.10; Luxury Package Testing & Validation Labs 2.35; Fragrance & Cosmetic Product Testing Labs 2.40.
**Reasoning:** A2LA showed stronger local evidence for packaging/package testing and broader cosmetic/product testing than pure fragrance testing; distributor and supplier lanes require target proof and may be lower margin or inventory heavy.
**Pattern:** #pattern/service-workflow-over-product-supply

## Learnings
When Kay surfaces luxury-adjacent niches, evaluate whether the investable version is a repeatable technical/service workflow rather than a product, inventory, or distribution business. If a narrow phrase such as "fragrance testing" has weak target-density proof, broaden only when the broadened category preserves the original customer wedge and improves search-fund viability.
