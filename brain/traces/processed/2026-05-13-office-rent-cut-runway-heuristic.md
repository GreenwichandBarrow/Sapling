# Decision Trace: Office Rent Cut — Stack Math, Not Standalone Heroics

## Context
Kay asked, mid-budget-review: "if I stopped renting the office space now, would it help the runway?" The April budget run had runway at 7.1mo from May 1, ~2mo short of the Feb 2027 deadline, with a $4,325/mo total savings target.

## Decisions

### How to frame the office-rent cut
**Chosen:** Quantify it as one piece of a stack, not a silver bullet. Heuristic surfaced: **each ~$2K/mo cut ≈ ~1 month of runway**; ~$1K/mo office rent ≈ ~2 extra weeks, closing ~23% of the $4,325/mo gap. Recommend YES but **route into Friday's expense-cut review as part of the stack**, not as standalone action. Flag the unknown that changes the math: month-to-month vs lease term / breakage fee.
**Reasoning:** Decision-fatigue doctrine + CFO discipline — a single cut framed in isolation invites a yes/no on the wrong question. The right question is the composition of the full $4,325/mo stack and the lease-term constraint, which belongs in the dedicated Friday review with all cuts visible together.
**Pattern:** #pattern/cut-as-stack-not-standalone-heroics

## Alternatives Considered
- Recommend the cut immediately as a standalone win — rejected: ~2 weeks alone doesn't move the deadline; decision is better made against the full stack.
- Defer the whole question to Friday with no math — rejected: Kay asked a quantitative question now; she gets the number now, the *decision* batches to Friday.

## Why This Trace Matters
Future CFO-style runway questions ("would cutting X help?") should get: (1) the per-$2K ≈ 1-month conversion immediately, (2) the specific blocking unknown named, (3) routing into the batched expense review rather than a standalone approve/reject. Answer the math in the moment; batch the decision into the stack.

## Key Insight
For incremental cost cuts against a runway gap, deliver the marginal-runway math instantly but defer the decision into the consolidated expense review — and always name the contract constraint (lease term / breakage) that determines whether the cut can land this month.
