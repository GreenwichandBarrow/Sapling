# Warm follow-up to a contact who already knows Kay — no event/conversation reminder

## Context
Drafting a follow-up to [[entities/matt-becky-colleague|Matt]] (met Kay at XPX, told Becky they had a great conversation, has a potential lead, swamped). Iterated v1 → final across five Kay corrections.

## Decisions

### Whether to anchor the follow-up on the prior XPX conversation
**AI proposed:** Open with / close on the XPX meeting and "continue our conversation," "pick it back up" (continuity framing).
**Chosen:** No reference to XPX or the prior conversation at all. He knows who Kay is and already told Becky it went well — a reminder reads as insecure and wastes the opening. Lead straight to the ask.
**Reasoning:** Kay flagged this twice ("no need to remind him about xpx"; then "I said we didn't need a reminder about xpx and you did it anyway" — "pick it back up" still implied it). Distinct from `feedback_continue_dont_reintroduce`: not just "don't re-introduce yourself" but "don't even use the prior event/convo as warm-up."
**Pattern:** #warm-followup-no-reintro

### CTA register
**AI proposed:** Soft close with an "or we can keep it open" option.
**Chosen:** One concrete goal — get a meeting on the calendar (June, given he's swamped); offer to send windows. No exit door. June framed as an offer, not as if already agreed.
**Reasoning:** Kay: "Don't offer to keep it open my goal is get something on the calendar" and "you are writing June as if you already asked." Soft accommodation is fine; the CTA must still convert.
**Pattern:** #warm-followup-no-reintro

## Learnings
For follow-ups to people who already know Kay and have signalled positive intent: cut all event/conversation callbacks, keep one light human touch (e.g., a mutual-contact mention), and drive to a concrete calendar ask with no open-ended exit. Accommodation language ("no pressure," push to a later month) is allowed only when it does not presume a prior ask and does not replace the concrete CTA.
