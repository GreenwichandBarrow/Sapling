## Alternatives Considered

1. **Send the wind-down notices this week per 5-20 plan.** Cost: locks in the wind-down decision before the network-mapping methodology has been tested at any volume. Both DealsX + JJ end ~late June regardless of what comes next.
2. **Run the pest 10-co effort in parallel with current channels, no decision criterion.** Cost: no forcing function — pest research drifts as "background" work; wind-down decision keeps deferring.
3. **Run pest 10-co as the EXPLICIT wind-down decision criterion (chosen).** Pest effort gets a hard deadline (6/30) + measurable outcome (NDA/conversation/positive-reply thresholds) + binary verdict. Channels keep running through June as parallel benchmark.

Option 3 chosen because it converts "wind down by summer" from a plan into a falsifiable hypothesis. The 10-co methodology embodies the 5-20 thesis convergence ([[user-kay-women-led-purpose-throughline]] + [[feedback-industry-is-output-of-network]]); the June experiment tests whether network-mapping outperforms mass-channel outreach in measurable terms.

## Reasoning

The performance data ALONE supported wind-down (0/0/0 outputs after months of ramp). What was missing: evidence that the proposed REPLACEMENT (women-led network 10-co methodology) would actually outperform.

Without a benchmark, winding down DealsX + JJ in June then under-performing on the pest effort would be a 1-2 month visibility gap on deal flow with nothing to show. The experiment makes the wind-down conditional on demonstrated replacement performance.

The 6/30 verdict mechanics:
- NDA is the highest-confidence pipeline signal (3x weight); pest is small-volume + relationship-heavy so even 1 NDA = strong signal
- Meaningful owner conversation is the second-tier signal (2x); requires both network access + Kay's voice resonating
- Positive-reply rate is the floor signal (1x); 30% threshold filters spam-acknowledgments

Cost of the experiment: 4-week sprint of personalized first touches + follow-ups, no Apollo spend, leverages existing 7-firm seed + 3 warm-intro asks. Cheap relative to the wind-down stakes.

**Wind-down decisions need a measurable counter-hypothesis before they fire.** "Channel X isn't working, drop it" is incomplete without "and channel Y will do better, measurably." The pest 10-co experiment forces the counter-hypothesis to prove itself before old channels get cut.
