# Convert expert contacts into owner-introducers

## Trigger
In a 5/31 `/socrates` session, Kay named the real reason her warm network has not produced deal flow: "my network has made many introductions to industry experts, but not introductions to business owners. That has been the biggest selling block." Separately, a specialty-insurance small-business owner had called her unprompted to check in and offered "to review leads."

## Decision
- Treat the sourcing problem as a **layer problem, not a cold-vs-warm problem.** An advisory network (Stern/Cornell alumni, insurance experts, art/fashion advisors) structurally produces advice, not companies.
- When an **owner-level** contact engages, the high-value ask is to convert them from reviewer to **introducer**: ask them to name ONE specific owner (someone in their book slowing down, or a peer they'd personally vouch for) rather than to evaluate Kay's existing leads.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Keep using the network for lead review / validation.** Rejected: an owner's judgment on your leads is the least valuable thing they have; their roster of fellow owners is the most valuable. Using them as a sounding board wastes the asset.
- **Abandon warm channels, go build an owner-layer network from scratch (owner peer groups, trade associations).** Valid and complementary, but slower; the immediate, free move is to re-aim the contacts already engaging.

## Reasoning
Owners cluster with owners; advisors cluster with advisors. A specialty-insurance owner's client book is by construction a roster of business owners in the niches he insures — he sits on top of the exact layer Kay can't otherwise reach. The blocker was never warmth; it was that the asks were pointed at the advisor layer, which can't output owners no matter how warm.

## Why This Trace Matters
A future agent (or relationship-manager / warm-intro-finder skill) seeing an owner-contact check in will default to routing it as a lead-review or nurture touch. This decision says: route it as an **owner-introduction request for one named owner**. Without this, the system keeps mining an advisory network for owners it structurally cannot produce, and the deal-flow drought continues.

## Key Insight
Experts give advice; owners give owners. Re-aim warm contacts by layer: ask owner-level contacts for ONE specific owner intro, not for validation. Application of [[feedback_industry_is_output_of_network]] + [[feedback_bias_yes_on_introductions]]; captured in [[project_insurance_pivot_strategy]].
