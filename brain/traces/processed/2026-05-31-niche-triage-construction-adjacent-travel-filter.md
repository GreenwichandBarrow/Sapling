# Niche triage: construction-adjacent + travel-intensive soft excludes

## Trigger
Reviewing candidate niches mined from *The ETA Database by Acquiring Minds*, six were surfaced to Kay. She rejected three on grounds not present in the written exclude list: **trade-show exhibit design/manufacturing** (travel), **cultured marble / manufactured stone** (reads as construction), and **custom garage door & gate** (reads as construction).

## Decision
- REJECT trade-show exhibit niche — "it will have a lot of travel."
- REJECT cultured marble and garage door/gate — both "feel construction," especially in the NY market.
- KEEP/queue AED servicing, EHS compliance, aerospace/defense (none trip either filter).

## Alternatives Considered
- **Surface all six and let the Tuesday scorecard rank them.** Rejected: the DB tagged cultured marble "Manufacturing" and garage doors "Home Services," so a literal-exclude screen would have passed them through as fits and wasted one-pager + scoring effort.
- **Treat only literal NYC-construction as excluded** (the written rule). Rejected: Kay's lens is broader than the written rule.

## Reasoning
Kay pattern-matches *construction-adjacent* businesses (building products, installation/repair trades) to "construction" even when a database tags them manufacturing or home services — and the aversion is not NYC-specific, it is the category. Separately, travel-intensity is a reflexive disqualifier at the niche-screen stage (consistent with her travel-heavy-deal aversion, but applied earlier — kill the niche, not just decline a deal).

## Why This Trace Matters
The written buy-box excludes only "NYC construction." A future agent screening niches would not predict that cultured-marble manufacturing or garage-door service — cleanly B2B, recurring, fragmented — get rejected. These soft excludes must be applied at niche identification, before building one-pagers, or the pipeline burns effort on candidates Kay will reject on sight.

## Key Insight
Beyond the literal excludes, Kay reflexively rejects two niche categories: **construction-adjacent** (building products + trades, geography-agnostic, not just NYC) and **travel-intensive**. Apply both at the niche-screen gate. Codified in [[feedback_niche_screen_soft_excludes_construction_adjacent_travel]].
