# Decision Trace: Reach Feb 2027 Without a Salary Cut

## Context
Fund runway (Apr-30 close ~$190.5K, net steady-state burn ~$21K) showed a ~2-month shortfall vs the Feb-2027 deadline. Kay's investor *suggested* she pause her salary for a few months to extend runway. The salary line (~$13.7K/mo all-in) is by far the single largest lever — pausing it over-solves the gap.

## Decisions

### How to close the runway gap
**AI proposed:** Surfaced the salary pause as the decisive lever (partial or full, 3-month, defer-and-accrue) since wind-downs alone fall ~2 months short.
**Chosen:** Close the gap with **cost levers** — DealsX (~$1.58K) + JJ (~$1.1K) off, office summer pause/cancel (~$1K), discretionary trim ~$600/mo — and keep **salary untouched as an emergency backstop only.**
**Reasoning:** Kay manages the fund herself ("it was just a suggestion from them; they leave it to me"). She has a tight personal budget and is not willing to cut salary unless forced. The cost levers + travel discipline get her to Feb 2027 on a total-cash basis (~$6.7K to spare); the $40K DD reserve only binds if she's mid-diligence, which would bring its own capital/timeline.
**Pattern:** #pattern/cost-levers-over-salary-cut

## Learnings
- **Do NOT push the salary-pause recommendation as the default for Kay.** An investor floated it; Kay manages the fund and treats salary as a near-fixed personal need. Frame salary as the *emergency backstop*, not the primary lever.
- Sequence levers Kay controls (vendor wind-downs, office, discretionary) before anything touching her draw.
- Plan of Record committed to Budget Dashboard `Runway Forecast` tab. See [[project-dealsx-jj-windown-by-summer]], [[project-bookkeeper-kick-blocked-by-cpa]].
