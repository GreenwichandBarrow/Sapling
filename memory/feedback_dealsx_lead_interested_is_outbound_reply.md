A "Lead Interested For Greenwich and Barrow" email from DealsX / Prospect Geni (`dealsx.notifaction@gmail.com`) is a notification that a recipient of **DealsX's cold-OUTBOUND sequence run on G&B's behalf replied with interest**. It is NOT an inbound/unsolicited lead. Never characterize it as "inbound."

**Why:** DealsX is cold-email infrastructure ([[feedback_dealsx_is_cold_email_infra]]) — it sends outbound for G&B. An "interested lead" notification = the cold sequence converted a recipient into an engaged reply. Calling it "inbound" mis-states the funnel stage and undersells the signal (an outbound reply is warmer/stronger than a cold inbound). Kay corrected this 2026-05-18 (Greg Bruyere / tristate-stl.com).

**How to apply:** In briefings and routing, frame DealsX "Lead Interested" items as "DealsX outbound reply — {name} responded interested" → route to deal-evaluation + Attio as an engaged-reply on the DealsX channel. The notification arriving in Kay's inbox is a delivery mechanism, not evidence of inbound origin — judge funnel stage by substance, not by which inbox the alert lands in.
