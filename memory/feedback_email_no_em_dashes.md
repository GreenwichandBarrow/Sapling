Do NOT use em dashes (—) in any written copy for Kay. The rule applies to EVERYTHING she signs her name to or presents externally: emails, investor decks, CIM slides, briefs, public artifacts, LinkedIn posts. Originally captured for emails; expanded to all written copy on 2026-05-28 during pest CIM deck slide 6 revision.

**Why:** Kay does not use em dashes in her writing voice. They read as unnatural to her.

**How to apply:**
- Replace em dashes with periods, commas, semicolons, parens, or line breaks
- Bullet style: instead of "X (Oct 2026) — Y. Z." write "X (October 2026). Y. Z."
- Subtitle/closing style: instead of "A — B — C" write "A. B. C." or "A, B, and C."
- Source line style: instead of "Hoffman, William — NJPMA 4/29/26" write "Hoffman, William (NJPMA, 4/29/26)"
- Scope: applies to deck text, brief text, email text, vault note user-facing prose, anything Kay reads or sends. Internal-only code comments / debug output are fine.
