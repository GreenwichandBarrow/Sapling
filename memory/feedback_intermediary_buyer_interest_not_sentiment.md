When emailing a sell-side intermediary or investment banker, frame every ask as a **buyer expressing concrete interest in the opportunities they are mandated to sell** — not as personal reflection or sentiment.

**Why:** Selling businesses is the intermediary's job. Sentiment language ("our conversation stayed with me," "I keep thinking about it") reads as soft and unserious to someone whose currency is buyer intent. Kay corrected this on the 2026-05-18 [[entities/carlos-nieto-dca|Carlos Nieto]] / DCA draft: *"its not about they stayed with me… he advises sell side, its its his job to sell businesses. You say I am interested in learning more about the opportunities you mentioned."*

**How to apply:**
- Use "I'm very interested in learning more about the opportunities you mentioned: [name them specifically]."
- Be specific and actionable so the intermediary knows exactly how to act (which deal, what next step — teaser, intro, status).
- Personal warmth still belongs in the opener/closer (especially family-close contacts), but the *ask* is buyer-to-seller, not sentimental.
- On their reply, the named opportunities route into the active deal pipeline (deal-evaluation intake, sourced via that intermediary). See [[feedback_pe_rollup_relationship_is_exit_channel_not_dealflow]] for the one exception class.
