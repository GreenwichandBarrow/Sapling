When drafting the canonical THANK YOU template's `{call_callback}` slot for intermediaries (brokers, IBs, sell-side lawyers, CPAs, intermediary referrals), do NOT echo the counterparty's most-emphasized point in the call as Kay's takeaway.

**Why:** Brokers signal-test buyers in first calls. The thank-you is the first written artifact post-call. Parroting their thesis back signals Kay was learning, not comparing notes — and subtly undercuts any peer-level endorsement they offered in-call. Kay rejected this on the 2026-05-22 Sam Curcio thank-you when "Your framing on speed as the #1 differentiator…" was proposed: *"I don't think I like the speed mention."* Sam had hammered speed as his central thesis AND told Kay *"you're doing more than most people I meet"* — echoing speed undercut the second.

**How to apply:**

- Screen any proposed `{call_callback}` against: *would the counterparty recognize this as restating their own central pitch?* If yes → parrot. Swap.
- Pick a substantive market/structural observation Kay can hold a peer-level view ON, not one she received FROM. Examples that work for sell-side intermediaries: PE saturation read on a specific size band, fragmentation observation in a vertical, financing-structure dynamic. Examples that fail: "speed matters most," "inquiry volumes," "bid-process discipline" — those are the broker's own toolkit.
- When unsure, drop the callback entirely. The template tolerates a tight version: *"Thanks for taking the time today. Really helpful conversation."* Light is safer than parrot.
