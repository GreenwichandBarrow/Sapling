Investor call preps are short, bottom-line, audience-focused.

**Canonical structure — 7 sections, ≤1 page (Jeff Stevens 4/22/26 = reference template, `brain/briefs/2026-04-22-jeff-stevens-call-prep.md`):**

1. **Header** — `{Investor} Call Prep. {Date}` / cadence + call # + days since last + investor role
2. **AI Operations** — ops/build status + strategic narrative (this is where thesis evolution lands as a second paragraph if it's the cycle's news; do NOT split into separate "Thesis evolution" + "Methodology shift" sections)
3. **Conferences.** — 2-3 line summary
4. **Interesting conversations** — single dense paragraph listing names + 1-line context, plus any takeaway tying back to prior call's framing
5. **Active Niches** — `{counts by status}. Silent focus on top-2.` followed by `1. {Niche}` / `2. {Niche}` / `3-N. Rest of stack per tracker`. Three lines total. **Never enumerate the full stack** — it eats page space and dilutes signal.
6. **Focus ask for this period.** — 3 short lines
7. **Upcoming.** — 3-4 short lines
8. **Questions for Jeff.** — 3-4 questions

**Why:** Live correction 2026-05-26 — Kay showed two side-by-side screenshots. The compact 4/22 format (~1 page) is the standard. The 5/22-generated 5/27 brief drifted: enumerated all 8 active niches as indented list, added "Thesis evolution" + "Methodology shift" as standalone sections with dense paragraphs, added a trailing recap section. Read as a strategy memo instead of a status update. The proper format reads as a status update with strategy implied.

**How to apply:**
- No team mentions (investors already know the team).
- No deal history recaps (they remember).
- Active Niches: top-2 named, rest as `3-N. Rest of stack per tracker`. Never the full enumerated list.
- Major thesis pivots fold into AI Operations as a second paragraph, not their own section.
- No trailing recap sections (summit takeaways, webinar reminders) — absorb into Conferences / Interesting conversations.
- Mention the AI operational system and calibration loop as productivity proof.
- Tailor the Questions section per investor (their specific network ask, their prior framing).
- No em dashes — use periods, colons, parens, semicolons.
- Length test: if the brief spills past ~1 page in Google Docs default, cut.
