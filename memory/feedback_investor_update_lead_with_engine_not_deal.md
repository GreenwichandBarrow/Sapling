For Kay's quarterly investor updates, **lead with structural conviction — what she controls and has built — not with deal-proximity.** The Q1 2026 headline converged (via /socrates, 2026-06-03) on **"engine built, edge identified"** rather than "closer to closing a deal." A specific live deal is framed as *evidence of inventory the engine produces*, never the headline.

**Why:**
- A single live deal is a fragile proof base — vetoes surface late and deal-specific. The Q1 ag-tech deal died on a main-investor Colombia-geography veto, collapsing a 2-deal proof base to 1. Anchoring the quarter's narrative on a deal that can die recreates the over-promise/under-deliver trap (promise it, miss, owe another explanation in 90 days).
- "Closer to a deal" is the claim every searcher makes every quarter forever; it doesn't differentiate and it's not believable from prior-update readers.
- Per [[feedback_court_case_psychology_identity_layer]], Kay's identity-to-performance wiring turns each update into "courtroom evidence" → avoidance (this is part of why updates go overdue). A headline keyed on an uncontrollable (will a deal close?) feeds the trap; a headline keyed on controllables (engine built, field cadence hit, edge identified) is defensible regardless of deal outcome. Under-promise on what she can't control, over-deliver on what she can.

**How to apply:**
- Headline = structural/controllable progress (engine, edge, cadence, network depth). Deals appear as by-product inventory, not the lead claim.
- **Don't apologize for gaps or frame a skipped quarter as defense.** Reframe as agency (deliberate pause to reflect + build). Let the dates do the work; don't name "six months since last update."
- Carry the deal-discipline learning into the deck as a START bullet: **pre-vet deals against investor hard constraints (geography, sector, structure) before deep diligence** — surfaces vetoes early.
- This is a durable framing default for the investor-update skill (quarterly mode), promotable via `evolve`. Related: [[feedback_quasi_decline_via_investor_hesitation]], [[project_g_b_doctrine_kristin_lessons]] (investors ≠ therapists), [[feedback_investor_prep_format]].
