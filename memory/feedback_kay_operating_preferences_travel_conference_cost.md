Operating preferences Kay confirmed in the 2026-05-21 /socrates session. These shape conference selection, network mapping, and sourcing-channel decisions, and reinforce the runway/cost-lever discipline (see [[project-dealsx-jj-windown-by-summer]]).

- **Travel minimized — long-standing and load-bearing.** Work travel kept to occasional; personal travel stays separate. This is not a recent reaction: Kay turned down senior Chanel roles (head of strategy, head of fashion) specifically because they travel constantly. Family weighs in favor of staying local.
- **Cheap/small conferences outperform expensive/large ones.** Concrete data Kay cited: **XPX Deals event ($90) was much more effective than InsurTech ($1,695).** Smaller group gatherings produce better signal. Cost is not a proxy for value here — the inverse has held.
- **NY-centric sourcing, framed as a positive.** Kay is in NY and "everyone comes here." Concentrate sourcing energy in NY/the tri-state corridor; favor local chapters over national HQ when tapping women's industry organizations.
- **Family-first** is the constant underneath all three.

**Why:** these are durable constraints, not session-specific, and Kay has sacrificed senior career roles to hold them. Treating travel-heavy or expensive-conference options as defaults will get them pruned every time.

**How to apply:**
- Conference selection (conference-discovery) defaults to **NY-based, smaller, lower-priced**. Do not surface travel-heavy or high-ticket conferences without a specific reason.
- Decline travel-intensive niches/deals reflexively (already a soft-exclude — see [[feedback-niche-screen-soft-excludes-construction-adjacent-travel]]); frame declines via investor-hesitation where useful (see [[feedback-quasi-decline-via-investor-hesitation]]).
- When sourcing/network-mapping, prioritize NY-accessible networks.
