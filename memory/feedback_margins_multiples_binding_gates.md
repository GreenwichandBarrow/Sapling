2026-06-03 strategic reframe (Kay's own, surfaced mid School-of-Hard-Knocks niche exploration). This is load-bearing for all future niche/deal screening.

**The wall:** Anywhere a business is genuinely good (healthy margins + recurring revenue + growing industry), PE has already arrived and bid the entry multiple past a search-fund-winnable price (the 35% IRR breaks). In Kay's words, "I can't win." This is the same reason **insurance brokerage** died (10-14x multiples) and **pest** — her women-network niche — "isn't proving out": multiples wall, different address. The three things that make a business attractive to her are the exact three that make PE bid it up.

**Binding gates = MARGINS + MULTIPLES.** Fragmentation and recurring revenue are necessary-not-sufficient. Lead every niche screen with PE-saturation / entry-multiple + margin, NOT with "it's fragmented and recurring." A clean recurring business that PE has rolled up (e.g., submetering, facilities, HOA at $1M+ EBITDA, permit expediting) is a reject regardless of how good it looks.

**Two-discounts framework:** a business is cheap enough for Kay to win only if (a) **too small** for PE to bother, or (b) **too ugly** for PE to want. Thin margins are ruled OUT as the acceptable ugly. The rare third discount = a legacy-minded owner who takes less to sell to the right buyer (a woman who'll keep the name/people) — a needle in a haystack, which is *why* Kay leans toward a broker/opportunistic deal-flow channel rather than deep industry thesis.

**Survivors survive for structural reasons:** they're too-new/regulatory (building energy compliance — NYC LL97 forces recurring demand, PE hasn't reached the small end) or early-innings (reserve studies). The best-recurring niches are the most picked-over.

**Edge ≠ economics:** a domain-expert investor, a co-lead, or a warm network helps SOURCE (off-market access, certainty, legacy fit) and breaks ties — but it is **never a go/no-go upgrade**. It does NOT lower the multiple, fix the margin, or advance a niche past the margins+multiples gate, and it never substitutes for a formal disposition step (no auto-advance on a strong signal). Confirmed across 3 surfaces 2026-05-31→06-03: HOA had a domain-expert co-lead (Guillermo) + confirmed women-led network and still held on the multiples wall ([[2026-06-03-investor-edge-doesnt-override-economics]]); warm network keeps yielding advisors-not-owners, a layer problem not a warmth problem ([[2026-05-31-convert-expert-contacts-to-owner-introducers]]); strong signals don't auto-advance, disposition is a formal step ([[2026-06-03-niche-disposition-not-from-chat]]).

**Why:** prevents re-chasing structurally-walled niches and re-learning the same lesson (it cost a year of deep 30-niche scoring).

**How to apply:** screen niches on **PE-activity-and-margins FIRST**. A niche clears only if margins are normal (~10%+, see [[Pre-LOI deal screen — $300K Kay salary + 15%+ margins + 35% IRR]]) AND it is not already PE-saturated at the buy-box size. Healthy-margin + not-yet-PE-saturated + women-network = the real (narrow) frontier. Channel split: thesis for conferences, opportunistic for brokers ([[camilla-approach-jun2026]]).
