At the niche-identification gate, Kay applies two reflexive soft excludes that are NOT in the written buy-box exclude list. Apply them BEFORE building one-pagers or scoring, or the pipeline wastes effort on candidates she rejects on sight.

1. **Construction-adjacent** — building products and installation/repair trades read as "construction" to Kay even when a database tags them "Manufacturing" or "Home Services." Examples she rejected (2026-05-31): cultured marble / manufactured stone (tagged Manufacturing), custom garage door & gate (tagged Home Services). This is **geography-agnostic** — broader than the literal [[feedback_nyc_construction_hard_exclude]] (which only names NYC construction). The aversion is to the category, anywhere.

2. **Travel-intensive** — niches whose operating model requires significant travel get killed at the screen. Example: trade-show exhibit design/manufacturing (rejected 2026-05-31, "a lot of travel"). This is the niche-stage analog of her travel-heavy-deal aversion ([[feedback_quasi_decline_via_investor_hesitation]]) — but applied earlier: kill the niche, don't just decline a deal diplomatically.

**Why:** the written excludes (US-only, no CA/PE-owned/lending/aviation-software/NYC-construction/carve-outs) are under-inclusive of Kay's actual screening reflexes. DB-tagged "Manufacturing"/"Home Services" niches can still be construction-adjacent.

**How to apply:** when surfacing niche candidates, pre-filter out construction-adjacent (building products, trades) and travel-intensive models. If a borderline one is genuinely strong, flag the adjacency explicitly and let Kay call it — don't silently surface as a clean fit.
