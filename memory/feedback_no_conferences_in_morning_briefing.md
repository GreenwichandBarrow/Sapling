Conference and networking-event decisions (register / attend / evaluate) do NOT belong in the morning Decisions briefing. They route to the **Conference Pipeline tracker**, which Kay reviews on **Mondays**. Kay (2026-06-03), on an ACG NY DealMaker June-11 registration item surfaced in the Wednesday briefing: *"you shouldn't have conference recommendations in here — that goes on the tracker and I review it on Mondays, please leave out of here."*

**Why:** Conference triage is a batched Monday ritual against the tracker, not a daily ad-hoc decision. Surfacing individual conference invites in the daily briefing duplicates that review and adds decision-fatigue noise on non-Monday days.

**How to apply:**
- When email-intelligence / deal-aggregator surfaces a conference or networking-event invite, route it to the Conference Pipeline tracker (conference-discovery owns this) — do NOT add it as a Decisions-briefing item.
- This holds every day except as part of the **Monday** conference-discovery status check, where conference review is the explicit overlay.
- Exception unchanged: a confirmed external 1:1 *meeting* tomorrow still gets a brief-generation item (that's meeting prep, not conference triage).
