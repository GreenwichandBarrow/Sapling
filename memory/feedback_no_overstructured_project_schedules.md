When planning one of Kay's projects on a Gantt/project tab, do NOT impose a week-by-week milestone schedule with assigned due dates across the backlog. Capture the **core workstream** + any **standing daily cadence**, and leave inherited verification/setup milestones as an **unscheduled backlog** worked opportunistically.

**Why:** 2026-05-17 deal-aggregator planning. Claude proposed sequencing ~13 Gantt milestones into Week 1 / Week 2 with due dates. Kay: "everything on week 2 sounds like over structuring." The project's actual substance was two things — (1) create & manage the sourcing list + contact many contacts to grow the funnel, (2) run the daily cadence (5 emails/day + LinkedIn) for the 2-week push. The rest were inherited generic scaffolding (dashboard-sync verifications, hygiene passes) that do not need calendar slots. Forcing them into a fake schedule is the same inherited-playbook over-structuring Kay prunes every time (cf. [[feedback_strategic_thresholds_need_grounding]]).

**How to apply:** For project planning — distill to the 1-2 real workstreams + daily/standing cadence, schedule only those. Backlog/verification items: list them, leave Due blank, label "unscheduled backlog," let them get pulled in as they naturally arise. Propose the stripped version FIRST; do not present a full milestone-by-week grid as the default. If Kay says a plan is "over structuring" / "too much," that means strip to core, not reshuffle. See [[feedback_dont_want_to_go_through_means_pivot]], [[feedback_collapse_thin_boundaries]].
