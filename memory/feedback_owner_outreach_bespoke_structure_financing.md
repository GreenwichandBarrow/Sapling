In direct-to-owner outreach, the financing/backing sentence must read as **"investors behind me who give me the flexibility to build a bespoke structure around what matters most to you, whether that's price, timing, your team, or how involved you stay after a sale."** NEVER "committed capital in place."

**Why:** Two reasons converge (Kay's correction, 2026-05-29 PA pest Rejigg pitch). (1) **Accuracy** — Kay's investors hold a first right of refusal, not pre-committed/escrowed capital; "committed capital" overclaims financing, and sellers/advisors test that claim in diligence. An overclaim that unravels costs trust. (2) **Differentiation** — the bespoke-structure framing is the real edge a personal searcher has over a strategic or PE buyer with a rigid playbook, and it leads with the owner's goals rather than a take-it-or-leave-it offer (consistent with leading with THEM, see [[feedback_outreach_about_them]]).

**How to apply:**
- Owner copy: convey backing WITHOUT overclaiming. "Investors behind me" ✓ / "committed capital," "fully funded," "fund," "lead investor" ✗ (also see [[feedback_never_say_fund]], [[feedback_no_revenue_in_outreach]]).
- Lead the financing line with the owner's goals: price, timing, their team, how involved they stay post-sale.
- This constraint is **owner-specific.** For industry advisors/intermediaries who are not sellers (e.g. Paul Giannamore at The Potomac Company), explicit "search fund / lead investor" framing is fine — see [[feedback_classify_intermediary_by_self_id]].
