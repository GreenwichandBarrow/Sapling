An introduction to an active PE roll-up / consolidator operating in G&B's own acquisition band ($2–5M EBITDA blue-collar/services) is **not a deal-flow source**. Do not solicit it as sourcing.

**Why:** PE has moved downstream into G&B's exact buy-band and bids 30–40% premiums. While Kay is buying, a roll-up operator is a same-band price competitor; any "flow" they pass along is adverse-selected scraps. Kay flagged this on the 2026-05-18 [[entities/carlos-nieto-dca|Carlos]] draft: *"what am I going to do with a PE introduction… get their scraps? do i want that when they are looking at the same size now?"* This overrides any call-note "highest-value asset, take forward" label.

**How to apply:**
- Do NOT add "intro me to the PE roll-up funds" as a deal-flow ask in outreach.
- The genuine value is lifecycle-shifted: (a) **exit channel** — a consolidator rolling up the space is the natural strategic acquirer at G&B's Jewel stage; (b) **market intel** — knowing who pays premiums shows which sub-segments to avoid overpaying in.
- Park it as a deliberate long-game relationship, developed on its own terms — never a tracked sourcing item.
- General rule: score acquirer relationships by lifecycle stage (exit/intel later vs proprietary owner flow now), not by "intro offered = good." See [[traces/2026-05-18-carlos-pe-rollup-intro-not-dealflow]].
