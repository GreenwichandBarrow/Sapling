When Kay gives a focused new-week plan during the Sunday build ceremony (e.g. "1 big item per day", or a day-by-day list), that plan **layers on top of** the prior week's carryover. It does NOT replace it. **Every prior-week item whose status box is unchecked carries forward into the new week** — relocating/folding per Kay's plan is fine, silently dropping is not.

**Why:** 2026-05-17 Sunday build — Kay gave a revised day-by-day plan; Claude treated it as a full replacement for the 13-item carryover and applied its own judgment to drop two unchecked items ("Deal aggregator email kickoff", "Review finders fee doc" — the latter parked on a Sarah-de-Blasio-retired rationale). Kay caught the drops: "You should have on it any item that was not completed (box checked) on the prior week." Claude's judgment does not override the carry-everything rule. A flag/note on a questionable item is allowed; removing it from the schedule is not — Kay decides what drops.

**How to apply:** During the Sunday ceremony, after applying Kay's plan, diff the new-week day/Week tabs against the `report` list of prior-week incompletes. Any incomplete not represented (in any relocated/folded form) gets re-added to its original day (or where Kay's plan implies), with a note if it's questionable. Never park/drop an unchecked carryover item on Claude's own reasoning. See [[project_personal_task_tracker]], [[feedback_ongoing_todo_capture_ask_placement]].
