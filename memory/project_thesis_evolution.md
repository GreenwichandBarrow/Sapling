## Phase 1: Education & Formation (Sep 2023 — Dec 2023)
- Learned search fund mechanics, PPM structure, cap tables, investor dynamics
- Initial target: $10M revenue, 25% EBITDA, recurring, recession-proof, service-based, high-end clients, NY
- Goal: grow to $60M revenue in 4 years, exit at 4x revenue
- Named the fund Greenwich & Barrow (replaced earlier "Willow Hill")
- Decided on search fund over independent sponsor or PE entry-level job

## Phase 2: Broad Niche Exploration (Jan — Jun 2024)
**Started with 3 core themes:** Commercial cleaning, Women's health, B2B support services

**Women's health deep dive:** Scored 30+ sub-niches. Top scorers:
- Medical billing & coding (28/30 — highest overall)
- Telemedicine services (26/30)
- IT services & EHR management (26/30)
- Egg banking, hormone therapy, concierge medicine, pelvic floor therapy all explored

**Key realization:** Not the clinical providers themselves, but the B2B compliance-themed services supporting them. "Compliance-as-a-Service" emerged as the intersection of women's health + BPO.

**Datacor model:** Acquire foundational B2B SaaS for women's health, expand via M&A (ERP/CRM play). Never found a viable target.

**Also explored:** Commercial cleaning (tabled — fragmentation but low differentiation), luxury property management, maternal health, sustainability consulting, 30+ other niches briefly screened.

## Phase 3: Fundraising & Close (Jul — Dec 2024)
- Fund structured as LLC, 10 units at ~$56.2K each ($562K target)
- Closed at ~$550K across 11 investors, 9.7 units (92% of target), budget shaved
- **Lead:** Anacapa Partners IV LP (20.7%, $114K) — JJ as lead investor
- **Co-lead:** Ashford Venture Partners (15.5%, $85K) — AS
- Plus 9 additional investors
- Key investor dynamic: lead investor concerned about support bandwidth, co-lead positioned to share load
- One institutional investor passed (over-penetrated in solo searchers)

## Phase 4: Search Launch & Infrastructure (Jan — Apr 2025)
- Launched mid-January 2025. Tech stack: HubSpot → later SalesFlare, Apollo, LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- 3 industry buckets formalized: (1) Healthcare/Women's Health B2B, (2) HNWI Infrastructure, (3) Tech/Software-Enabled Services
- Commercial cleaning maintained as strategic backup
- Scoring system: 9-metric scorecard (Growth CAGR, EBITDA margins, LTM growth, recurring revenue, retention, scalability, business quality, market position, management)
- Hired interns, set up office in co-working space with ~5 other searchers

## Phase 5: Art World Pivot (May — Sep 2025)
**Trigger:** ArtBinder founder conversation + UOVO storage call revealed viable art infrastructure thesis

**What worked:** Art collection management scored 2.75/3.0 on growth/penetration/catalyst. UOVO validated storage model (2+ year average stays, loyal clients).

**What didn't:** Art tech software had ceiling confirmed by ArtBinder founder ("lots of reluctance to pay"). Gallery count shrinking. Constellation/Volaris already acquired 3 biggest players.

**Key insight from ArtBinder founder:** "If she was going to acquire a business today she would choose a different industry." Advised: "Look for something that's a strong software run by nerds that don't understand marketing and branding."

**Art storage became primary**, with 4 targets identified: Artwork Archive, Catalogit, ArtSystems, Collector Systems

## Phase 6: Deals & Insurance Pivot (Aug — Dec 2025)
**Hangman Fine Arts (Deal 1):**
- Brooklyn-based, David Hurwitz (age 44), 80 employees
- Revenue: $600K→$1.5M→$4M→$6M→$7M (2020-2025). EBITDA $300-500K
- 90% client retention, proprietary inventory management software
- NDA signed, site visit conducted (investor Guillermo attended)
- **Passed:** 8% EBITDA margins, capital-intensive growth, investors uncomfortable

**Acumen (Deal 2):**
- Art storage/services, 6-7 partners, ~$13M revenue, ~$1.1M EBITDA
- Locations: NYC, LA, Miami. NDA signed, financials received
- Offer made at $5M, seller said lower than expected
- **Stalled:** Investors said too small, margins too thin for search fund economics

**Insurance brokerage pivot (Oct 2025):**
- Triggered by call with August Felker (insurance brokerage owner/searcher who validated thesis)
- Specialty insurance for fine art, luxury handbags, jewelry, wine, collectibles
- 25-35% EBITDA margins, 55% costs to people, high recurring revenue via renewals
- **BUT:** Double-digit purchase multiples (10-14x EBITDA) due to PE demand
- Richard (experienced operator) gave sobering assessment: quality targets "rare" and "well picked-over"

## Phase 7: Compliance Infrastructure & Retooling (Jan — Mar 2026)
**Thesis crystallized:** "Regulatory failure is expensive, boring, and unavoidable" — compliance-as-a-service, tech-enabled infrastructure

**Holding company vision:** Luxury-focused HoldCo spanning art storage, specialty insurance, concierge home services

**AI retooling:** Migrated entire stack (SalesFlare→Attio, Todoist→Motion, Fireflies→Granola, added Claude Code as orchestration layer). Harrison Wells (AI consultant) brought on.

**Current buy box (Mar 2026):**
- $2-5M EBITDA vertical software or tech-enabled B2B services
- $10-50M revenue ($5-40M for software)
- Durable recurring revenue, 10+ year history (5+ for software)
- Private, not PE-backed, transitioning from retiring/fatigued owner
- AI-enhanceable, not AI-disruptable
- Within 1 hour of home if on-site; anywhere US if remote

## Phase 8: Women-Led Throughline Convergence (May 2026)

**Trigger:** `/socrates` session 2026-05-20. Surface ask was "how do I close in 6 months." Underlying thread Kay self-surfaced mid-conversation: she chose Chanel post-MBA specifically for women-owned DNA + global empowerment; she pivoted Myself Renewed (her nonprofit) FROM women-in-labor TO women-owned women's health businesses; she has held an explicit post-exit goal of becoming a female investor in the search community; she became interested in pest only after discovering the women's pest management association; her interest in art storage collapsed when industry turned out male-dominated; software (the negative control) came and went with no female-led-network lock-in.

**The convergence:** female-led networks are the **load-bearing input** to industry selection, not a soft surfacing-order preference. The thesis becomes purpose-first: women-owned, recurring-revenue staples in NY, sourced via female-led networks. Pest primary (women's pest management association is the lock-in). Adjacent staple-female-skew industries (home healthcare, commercial cleaning, senior care, certain professional services) as warm-intro optionality. NOT classic women-owned consumer (fashion, retail, beauty, personal care). See [[user-kay-women-led-purpose-throughline]] and [[feedback-industry-is-output-of-network]].

Adapted: 3 buckets of 10 women-owned NY firms each + female-led-network triangulation + custom send + warm intro + coffee. Solo + analyst running the engine post-summer wind-down of DealsX and JJ (see [[project-dealsx-jj-windown-by-summer]]).

**What this Phase changes about prior phases:**
- Phase 2 women's-health exploration was correct attractor (woman-owned health) but wrong domain (chose to support clinical providers rather than acquire woman-owned businesses themselves). The thread was there.
- Phase 5 art world pivot dropped when art storage was discovered to be male-dominated. The throughline was operating.

**Why:** This history shows Kay's pattern: she starts broad, scores rigorously, pivots when data contradicts thesis, and converges toward compliance/infrastructure businesses serving luxury/UHNW clients — **and at Phase 8 finally claims an organizing principle that has been operating across her entire post-MBA career**.

**How to apply:** When evaluating new niches, reference the scoring history and kill reasons. Don't re-explore killed niches without new information. **As of Phase 8, the primary screen is "is there a female-led association or network in this industry that Kay can plug into in NY?" If no, expect the interest will not lock in regardless of financial fit.** Kay's strongest conviction is at the intersection of women-led-network + recurring-revenue staples + NY + female-owner-preferred + not-tech + not-travel-heavy.
