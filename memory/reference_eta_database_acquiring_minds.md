**The ETA Database by Acquiring Minds** — a curated spreadsheet of ~458 Acquiring Minds podcast guests who bought SMBs, tagged by Guest Name, Business Acquired, Industry, Acquisition Model, Guest Background, Country/State/City, B2B/B2C, SBA Loan, Multiple Acquisitions, Franchise, **Gender**, Episode #, etc.

- **Native Google Sheet (shared by will@acquiringminds.co):** `1BWA01te6NsRoP5te2FG7yBvaxFiq2lVKkTvyL_9OROI` — read via `gog sheets`. Tabs: Dashboard, About, **Raw Data** (the substance, headers row 1), Helpers.
- Kay's `.xlsx` copy lives in Drive folder `ACQUIRING MINDS` (`1VtGf3LiuG8p6V3kwpjgCwqTVT0uDxP22`).

**How G&B uses it (established 2026-05-31):**
- **Searcher-overlap heatmap.** Industry frequency = how crowded a niche is with searchers; per the buy-box, searcher overlap is a NEGATIVE. The mega-buckets (B2B Services ~97, Home Services ~65, Manufacturing ~50, Contracting ~42) are crowded — hunt at the sub-segment level, not the bucket. Lower-count, non-obvious niches with a closed deal = less competition + proof of path.
- **Female-buyer network seed.** Only ~37/458 (~8%) female — that scarcity is white space for Kay's women-led thesis ([[user_kay_women_led_purpose_throughline]]). The female buyers + their niches are warm-intro / peer candidates and proof-of-path.
- **Reality check on deal size.** 265 self-funded vs ~20 traditional search fund → this universe skews below G&B's $2–10M EBITDA box. Treat industries as directional signal, not a size benchmark.
