Kay's organizing principle across her entire post-MBA professional life is **supporting women in business** — women-led businesses, women-owned acquisition targets, women-led networks, and a post-search vision of returning to the search community as a female investor. This is not a search-fund-era preference. It runs from her career choices through philanthropy through search interests through stated post-exit goals.

**The throughline (surfaced 2026-05-20 in /socrates session):**

- **Fashion / luxury (pre-MBA + post-MBA career arc):** breeding ground for women-formed businesses. The "luxury" framing Kay has used has been a women-owned-business attractor disguised as a sector thesis.
- **Chanel (post-MBA job choice):** chosen *specifically* for the women-owned DNA and global empowerment focus. Kay loves the story that the 2.55 handbag was designed to free women's hands so they could "participate in life like a man."
- **Myself Renewed (Kay's nonprofit):** pivoted FROM women-in-labor TO **women-owned women's health businesses** — proactive directional shift toward women-owned.
- **Search-fund industry interests:** women's health (pelvic floor, doulas — female-owned attractor); insurance (entered via a woman who came from a woman-owned business); art storage (entered via a woman leader, *interest collapsed when industry turned out male-dominated* — cleanest evidence); pest (interest jumped when Kay discovered the women's pest management association); **software (the negative control — in-and-out interest for 15+ months, never locked, no female-led connection)**.
- **Active warm intros (women's leadership group, May 2026):** woman lender, woman super-connector lawyer, woman finance contact.
- **WSN gratitude (recorded 2026-05-20):** "grateful for women's communities, how supportive, how generous in helping."
- **Fundraising experience:** "I was disappointed by the lack of women" → explicit goal of returning post-exit as a female investor in the search community.

**Source of the purpose:** Kay's own words: *"My whole life I was talented and smart and I had to fight to be treated like the boys, or not to be overlooked."* That lived experience is the genesis. The purpose is durable across decades because it traces to identity-level lived experience, not positioning.

**Candidate purpose sentence (Kay sitting with it, not yet locked):**

> "My purpose is to acquire, operate, and eventually back women-owned businesses, because I have lived the experience of being overlooked as a woman, and the work of my career has been to build places where that does not happen."

**Why this matters:** prior memories (`feedback_women_network_priority`) framed women-priority as a soft surfacing-order lens within male-dominated industries. That framing was missing the load-bearing weight. The pattern is the **input** to industry selection itself, not a filter applied afterward. See [[feedback-industry-is-output-of-network]] for the structural reframe.

**How to apply:**
- When screening any new industry for Kay → first check "is there a female-led association/network we can plug into in NY?" If no, expect the interest will not lock in regardless of financial fit.
- When framing investor narrative / pitches / brand → use the throughline (Chanel → Myself Renewed → women-led search → female investor post-exit) as the spine. It is more legible than the industry-evolution story.
- When recommending partner / advisor / hire decisions → female-ownership is the lock-in for INDUSTRY INTEREST, NOT a proxy for partner reliability. Do not over-apply.
- When detecting "Kay seems to be spinning on industry" — check whether the candidate industries are women-led-network-connected. If not, the spin is because the lock-in is missing.
